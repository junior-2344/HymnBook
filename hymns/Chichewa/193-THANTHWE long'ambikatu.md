THANTHWE long'ambikatu

THANTHWE long'ambikatu,
Ndibisale momwemo!
Madzi ndipo mwaziwo,
Zotuluka m'nthitimo,
Zinditsuke m'mtima mbuu!
Zindilimbikitsetu.
Ntchito zanga zonsezo,
Sizikondweretsa 'Nu;
Ndikazigwiritsadi,
Ndikalira nsonizi,
Zonse sizikwanazi,
Koma Yesu yekhayo.
M'manja mopanda kanthu,
Mtanda ndingogwiradi;
'Maliseche mveke'ne,
Ndili ndekha, msunge'ne,
Ndithawire m'mtsinjewo,
Munditsuke m'mtimamo.
M'moyo uno kunsiku,
Pena ndimwaliratu;
Mmene ndidzakwerako,
Tiwonane masowo,
Thanthwe long'ambikatu,
Ndibisale momwemu.
(Mayiwe! Thanthwe long'ambikatu,
Ndibisale momwemu!)
