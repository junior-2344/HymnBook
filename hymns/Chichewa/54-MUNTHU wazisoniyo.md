MUNTHU wazisoniyo

MUNTHU wazisoniyo,
Tim'tcha dzina lakelo;
Mpulumutsi linanso,
Aleluya, ndiye Yesu.
Anapeputsidwako,
Nandifera pa mtanda;
Anandiwombolayo,
Aleluya, ndiye Yesu.
Ife tinachimwadi,
Iye sanachimwe'yi;
Anachotsa tchimoli,
Aleluya, ndiye Yesu.
Anapachikidwatu,
Natha ntchito yakeyo!
Anakwera 'mwambamo!
Aleluya, ndiye Yesu.
Adzabwera Iyenso,
Kudzatenga akewo;
Nyimbo tidzayimbanso,
Aleluya, ndiye Yesu.
