POMPANO pandikonda
Poyendayenda ’ne;
Paŵala mosekera
Ndi dzuŵa loti mbee!
Ndikondwa nsiku zonse
Ndikali m’dzikoli,
Potsata Yesu m’njira yake
Yonseyi.
Ulendo ungoyamba
Wakunka kwathuko;
Amati: “Udzapeza
Zakusautsa’mo”
Zobvuta ndi chisoni
Sindiziopa ’yi;
Ndidzangomtsata Yesu m’njira
Yonseyi.
Zoipa ndi zabwino
Zakundigwerazo,
Ndingonka nazo zonse
Ndimuze Yesuyo;
Zoŵaŵa adzachotsa
Zondiliritsazi;
Ndikangomtsata Yesu m’njira
Zondipingitsa zonse Zoloŵa m’mtimamo
Achotsa, nandisunga
M’ulendo wonsewu.
Sindidzaopa kanthu,
Ngakhale imfayo,
Pomtsata Yesu yekha kunka
Kwathuko.
