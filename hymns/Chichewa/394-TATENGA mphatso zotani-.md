TATENGA mphatso zotani?
Zothandiza ntchito yake;
Ya Yehova Mbuye wathu,
Tipereke mokondwera.
Tipereke kwa Mbuyeyo,
Monga wapatsa ifetu;
Zothandiza moyo wathu,
Tipereke mokondwera.
Tipereke mphatso zathu,
Tisam'mane Mbuye wathu;
Watipatsa zochuluka,
Tipereke mokondwera.
Mbuye wathu dalitsani,
Zopereka zathu izi;
Ngakhale zili zochepa,
Zigwiretu ntchito Yanu.
