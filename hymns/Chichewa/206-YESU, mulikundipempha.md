YESU, mulikundipempha
Munaterodi;
“Mwana, gonja, ndikupatsa
Chimwemwe.
Kale ndinakana Inu,
Ndinathaŵatu:
Ndinazengereza, ndati: “Maŵatu.”
Lero mtima ungolema,
Usaukadi;
Wina wakundimasula
Ndilibe.
Mbuye, ndingogonja tere,
Ndibvomerapo;
Mundithyole, mundikonze
M’mtimamo.
Chifuniro changa, Mbuye,
Ndiperekachi;
Mutsogole, ndidzatsata
Inudi.
Tere moyo wanga wonse,
Mawu, ntchitonso
Zidzakondweretsa Inu
Zonsezo.
E,chimwemwe chachikulu,
N’taperekadi
Mtima wanga, ndimapeza
Mtendere.
