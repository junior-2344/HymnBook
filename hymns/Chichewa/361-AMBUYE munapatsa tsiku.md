AMBUYE munapatsa tsiku
Limene langopitali,
M’mamaŵa nyimbo zathu zomwezo
Mukhale nafe nthaŵiyi
Mukhale, mukhale,
Mukhale nafe nthaŵiyi;
Mukhale, mukhale,
Mukhale nafe nthaŵiyi.
Tipempha kuti Mpingo wanu
Ukhale ponse m’dzikoli;
Mumdima monse muŵaletu,
Kuŵala kwanu kudzedi.
M’maiko monse, zisi zomwezo,
Kuŵala kumafikanso;
Kupempha sikuleka konse
Kuyamikira Yesuyo.
Pogona ife, amadzuka
Abale akutaliwo;
Panthaŵi yonse anthu ena
Alemekeza Mbuyeyo.
Ufumu wanu, Mbuye Yesu,
Ukhale nthaŵi zonsedi;
Magulu onse adzagonje,
Naloŵe m’njira mwanumu.
