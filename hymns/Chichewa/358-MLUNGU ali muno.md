MLUNGU ali muno

MLUNGU ali muno,
Tonse timgwadire,
Mantha omwe timchitire;
Ali mukachitsi,
Tonse tili chete,
Ndi ulemu timpembedze;
Ndiye yekha Mlungu wathu
Atipulumutsa,
Tidzamlemekeza.
Mwini madalitso,
Mndikonzere m’mtima,
Mbuye, ndingokhulupira.
M’Mwambamo angelo
Agwadira inu,
Ife tigonjanso kuno;
Mpingo wonse uchiitse
Chifuniro chanu.
Ponse pansi pano.
Yesu Mbuye wanga,
M’khale m’mtima mwanga,
Ndiwotu kachisi wanu.
Ikafika imfa,
Mundisunge kwanu
Kuli inu ndi angelo.
Moyo wanga ulimbike
Kufikira ine
Ndidzakupenyani.
