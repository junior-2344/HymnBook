LEMEKEZANI Yehova

LEMEKEZANI Yehova,
Antchito akenu;
M'mabwalo a nyumba yake,
Muimiliramo.
Lemekezani Yehova,
Pakuti ngwabwino;
Muyimbire zom'tamanda,
Mukondweretsa 'Ye.
Lemekezani Yehova,
Osankhidwa ake;
Musapembedze mafano,
Koma Mlungu yekha.
Lemekezani Yehova,
M'patseni ulemu.
Mumuyimbire Yehova
Kunthawi zonsezo.
