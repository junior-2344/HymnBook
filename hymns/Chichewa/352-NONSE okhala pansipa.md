NONSE okhala pansipa

NONSE okhala pansipa,
Muyamikire Mlunguyo;
Ndi nyimbo mtumikire ’Ye;
Mubwere mokondweratu.
Yehova ndiye Mlungudi,
Anatilenga yekhayo;
Amatidyetsa ake ’fe,
Ndi nkhosa zake ifetu.
Loŵani m’Nyumba mwakemu
Ndi nyimbo zokondweretsatu;
Mumyamikire Mlunguyo.
Mulemekeze Dzinali.
Pakuti Mbuye Mlunguyo
Wokoma mtima ndiyedi,
Woona adzakhalabe
Kunthaŵi nthaŵi zonsetu.
