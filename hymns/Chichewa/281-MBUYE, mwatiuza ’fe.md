MBUYE, mwatiuza ’fe

MBUYE, mwatiuza ’fe
Tizikhala mchere
Wokometsa onsewo
Akumanga kwathu.
Mcherewu ndi ifetu;
Mtifanize nanu,
Tikakope ndi chitsanzo
Onse akuchimwa.
Mbuye, mwatiuza ’fe
Tizikhala nyali
Yoonetsa onsewo
Njira yonka kwanu.
Nyali ndi ifetu;
Kuunika kwanu
Mutipatse tiŵalire
Osokera onse.
Tisasungaluke ’yi,
Tisasiye Yesu.
Tisabise nyaliyi
Pakuchita mantha.
Mcheretu ndi nyaliyi
Tizikhala tonse;
Mbuye, mtiyeretse m’mtima
Tikatero ndithu.
