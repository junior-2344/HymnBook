PAUSIKU uja,
Yesu anayenda,
M'njira yonka ku
Getsemani;
Kunali mdima bii,
Iye anagwada;
Pansi pa mtengo wazitona.
Anapempheradi,
Anapempheradi,
Atagwada yekhayekha;
Napempha chikhochi,
Chindipitilire;
Kufuna kwanu kuchitike.
Machimo a m'dziko,
Adam'lemera 'Ye,
Potuluka mu Getsemani;
Adapsinjidwadi,
Ndi zoipa zathu;
Zomwe zidam'tengera imfa.
M'tithandize Mbuye
Tisaiwaletu,
Zachikondi chanu pa ife;
Mudapempherera,
Zoipa zathuzo:
M'munda uja wa Getsemani.
Zochimwa zathuzo
Yesu anasenza,
Zinathera pamtanda wake;
Lero lino ife,
Tapulumutsidwa;
Ndi mwazi wake wa Yesuyo.
