AMBUYE Yesu anamva chisoni

AMBUYE Yesu anamva chisoni,
Chifukwa imfa inam'zungulira;
Anatenga ophunzira atatu,
Kukam'pempherera.
Moyo wanga,
Wazingidwa,
Ndi chisoni;
Cha kuimfa.
Yudasi yekha ndiye anazemba,
Kukayitanatu ankhondo ake;
Anabweratu ndi mikondo yawo,
Kukam'gwira Yesu.
Lero linonso oyipa tonsefe,
Tikum'pereka Yesu Ambuyeyo;
Tiyeni tilape machimo athu,
Atikhululukire.
