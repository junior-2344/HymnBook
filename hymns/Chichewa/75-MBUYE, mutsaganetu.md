MBUYE, mutsaganetu

MBUYE, mutsaganetu
Nane ndingachimwenso;
Nanu ndisungidwedi,
Mwa chikondi chanucho.
Mbuye Yesu,
Mundiperekeze inetu;
Ndine mlendo, Mbuye ‘Nu,
Mutsagane nanetu
Inu mundibisadi
M’mene ndabvutidwamo,
Ngati muli ndinedi
Bwino ndidzakhalamo.
Mbuye, mutsaganebe
Mpaka moyo watha zi!
Kwanu mundifitseko,
Kwanu nsoni zathadi.
