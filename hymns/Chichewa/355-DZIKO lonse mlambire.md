DZIKO lonse mlambire
Yesu Mbuye wathuyo,
Muimbire ndi chimwemwe
Dzinalo.
Mbwere nonse muone
Zomwe achita Yesu:
Nzozizwitsa, nzodabwitsa
Ntchitozo.
Aweruza ndi mphamvu
Ku nthaŵi yamuyaya;
Dizo lake lisamala
Am’dziko.
Mbwere mudzamve nonse
Zamphamvu za Yehova;
Mumwibire, mumyamike
Yehova.
Tisonkhane tonsefe,
Tigwadire Ambuye;
Timlambire, timlambire,
Yehova.
