MLENGI wathu Woyeranu

MLENGI wathu Woyeranu,
Mutimvere 'fe;
M'wasungire akutali,
Bwinodi.
Mbuye Yesu,
m'yandikire,
M'wakondwetsebe;
Mulimbitse mphamvu zawo,
Zonsezo.
Akawona masautso,
M'khale nawodi;
Akondwere mwachikondi,
Chanucho.
Mpulumutsi wakukoma,
Muwagwirize;
Nsiku zonse akulemekezeni,
(Akule-mekezeni.)
Mzimu wanu Woyerawo,
Awayeretse;
Agonjetse ndi chisomo,
M'nkhondoyi.
Tate, Mwana, Mzimu, ndi 'Nu,
Mlungu mmodzitu;
Muwasunge mfupi mwanu,
Ponsepo.
