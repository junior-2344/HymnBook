MULUNGU aŵakonda anthu

MULUNGU aŵakonda anthu
Ali pansi pano;
Afuna kuŵapulumutsa
Kumilandu yawo.
A! Chikondi chachikulu
Mulungu ali nacho,
Chimam’tengera Mpulumutsi,
Andifere pano
Ndikhulupira mwa Ambuye,
Mulungu namukitsa,
Imfayo indipulumutsa,
Mwazi und’yeretsa.
Chikondi chake chindidzera
M’mtima mwanga muno,
Chipulumutsa nditalema
Ndi machimo anga.
Mukondwe anthu a Mulungu,
Tere mulandira
Chimwemwe chija chakumwamba,
Chidzaloŵa m’mtima.
Mulungu atipatsa mphamvu
Yoletsetsa tchimo;
Pofika tsikulo la imfa,
Tidzakhala bwino.
