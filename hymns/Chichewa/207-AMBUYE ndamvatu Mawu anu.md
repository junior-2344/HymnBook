AMBUYE ndamvatu Mawu anu

AMBUYE ndamvatu Mawu anu,
Cholinga changa;
N'kalowe M'mwamba.
Kunyumba ndapemphera,
M'tchalitchi muno ndapemphera;
Cholinga changa,
N'kalowe M'mwamba.
Ambuye mundithandizetu ine,
Cholinga changa;
N'kalowe M'mwamba.
Ambuye lowani m'mtima mwanga,
Cholinga changa;
N'kalowe M'mwamba.
