GETSEMANI, Getsemani

GETSEMANI, Getsemani,
Ambuye Yesu,
Zedi napemphera:
Kuti chikhocho chim'pitilire,
Chili pafupi naye.
Bwerera iwe wokondedwa,
Goli la imfa, lamasulidwa;
Yesu afuna utembenuke,
Nutsate njira yake.
Pamtandapo, pamtandapo,
Ambuye Yesu,
Zedi napemphera;
Kupempherera anthu ochimwa,
Ndikuwapulumutsa.
Mbuye Yesu, Mbuye Yesu,
Afuna ife;
Ndithu tilapedi,
Zoipa zathu zomwe tichita,
Kuti tifike M'mwamba.
