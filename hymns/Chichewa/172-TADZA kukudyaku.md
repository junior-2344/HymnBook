TADZA kukudyaku

TADZA kukudyaku,
Chakonzedwatu;
Mkate wamoyodi, idyatu ’we;
Bwera wochimwanso,
Yesu adikatu,
Akuitanaku; mvetsatu ’we
Tadza kumtsinjeku
Wa moyo weni,
Chiza zoŵaŵazo za m’mtimamo;
Mtsinje wodzalawo,
Wokwaniratuwo
Yesa sakana ’yi; idzatu ’we.
M’dzina la Yesuyo
Pemphatu iwe,
Iye adzamvatu, dikirapo.
Wamphamvu Mlunguyo,
Ndiye apatsatu,
Dalo labwinolo; idzatu ’we
Yamba ulendowo
Wonka Kumwamba;
Pano mpoipatu padzikoli;
Komwe tikondwako,
Tidzasekeranso;
Tinke Kumwambako; idzatu ’we.
