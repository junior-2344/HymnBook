YENDANI munjira yake

YENDANI munjira yake,
Yendani m'kuunika kwake;
Yesu atitsogolera,
Tiyende, inu tiyende,
Yendani munjira yake.
Yesu atitsogolera,
Yesu n'kuunika kwathu;
Yendani, inde yendani,
Yendani munjira yake.
Yendani m'mapazi ake,
Yendani m'chiyero chake;
Yesu wathu patsogolo,
Tiyende, inu tiyende,
Yendani m'mapazi ake.
Yesu atitsogolera,
Yesu Mpulumutsi wathu;
Yendani, inde yendani,
Yendani m'njira yake.
