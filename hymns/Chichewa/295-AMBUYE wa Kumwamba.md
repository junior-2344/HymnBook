AMBUYE wa Kumwamba,
Mundithangatetu
Kuyenda bwino lomwe
M’ulendo unowu. Kaŵiri ndi kaŵiri
Ndipunthwa m’njiramo
Mlimbitse mtima wanga,
Ndingatayikeko.
Ndikhumba Mzimu wanu
Kundiyeretsa mbuu!
Pang’ono ndi pang’ono
Mundiphunzitsetu;
Ndifuna nzeru zanu
Zakundifitsako
Kumwamba kuli Inu,
Ambuye wangayo.
Yochita ndi yodikha
Mupatse mphamvuyo,
Ndikhale wochenjera,
Wogwira ntchitozo.
Zoipa zanga zonse
Mufafanizezo,
Ndiyende nanu nokha
Masiku onsewo.
