YESU, muli pa chimpando
Chakuŵala mbee! Kumwamba;
Mutipenye ife ana;
Mutimvere, Yesu.
Ife ana sitiopa,
Mukabwera mutiona,
Tamva kale mutikonda;
Mutimvere, Yesu.
Ife tikondana nanu,
Tiimbira nyimbo zanu,
Tiyamika mphamvu yanu;
Mutimvere, Yesu.
Lero mkhale nafe ndithu,
Poseŵera ndi pantchito,
Popemphera ndi poimba;
Mutimvere, Yesu.
Mutiletse tisaname
Ndikunena zotukwana,
Mtiphunzitse kukondana;
Mutimvere, Yesu.
