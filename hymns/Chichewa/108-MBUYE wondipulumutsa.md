MBUYE wondipulumutsa

MBUYE wondipulumutsa,
Mulandire mtima wanga;
Mwandipatsa Mzimu wanu,
Womeretsa zipatsozo.
Ndiye Mzimu Wakuyera,
Atipatsa ife makhalidwe;
Ofanana naye Yesu,
Anatsika Kumwambako.
Naye Mzimu m'ndiphunzitse,
Kukondwera nsiku zonse;
Ndi kukhala ndi mtendere,
Osaderatu nkhawa'yi.
M'ndithandize kukondana,
Ndi kuthangatira onse;
Ndi kukhululukirabe,
Akundichimwirawo.
M'ndilimbitse ndipilire,
Osadandawula konse;
Ndi kulamulira thupi,
Ndi zofuna zakezo.
Kuzilala ndi kufatsa,
Inde, zonse muzikonda;
M'ndiphunzitse pondipatsa,
Mzimu wanu Woyerawo.
