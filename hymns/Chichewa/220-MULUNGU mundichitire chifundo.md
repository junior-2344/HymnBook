MULUNGU mundichitire chifundo

MULUNGU mundichitire chifundo,
Monga mwakukoma mtima kwa Inu;
Mufafanize machimo angawo,
Mwaunyinji wansoni zokomazo.
Mubwereze kunditsukane,
Mphulupulu zanga zonsezo;
M'ndiyeretse kundichotsera,
Machimo anga onsewo.
Ndazindikira machimo angawo,
Ndipo choipa chili chikhalire;
Pa Inu, Inu nokha ndinachimwa,
Pamaso panu ndachita tchimolo.
Mundiyeretse, ndipo ndidzayera,
Munditsuke, ndipo ndidzakhala mbuu!
Musanditayetu pamaso panu,
Musandichotsere Mzimu Woyera.
