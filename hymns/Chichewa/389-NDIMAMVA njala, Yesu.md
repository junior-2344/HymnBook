NDIMAMVA njala, Yesu

NDIMAMVA njala, Yesu,
Ya mtima wakusoŵa;
Mdidyetse ngati mana
Anagwa m’chipululu.
Ndimamva ludzu, Yesu
Muloŵe mwanga lero;
Mndimwetse ngati madzi
Anatuluka m’mwala.
Ndi mkate wanu, Yesu,
Ndi thupi lobvulala,
Mndilimbikitse, Mbuye
Mundikhutitse mtima.
Mwa chikho chokumbutsa
Chikondi chopambana,
Mndipatse moyo wanu,
Ndikayanjane nanu.
Mndidyetse m’chipululu
M’ulendo wanga wonse;
Mdimwetse, ndipirire
Wosalefuka konse.
