PALIBE wina Kumwamba

PALIBE wina Kumwamba,
Ndinutu Mbuyanga;
Pakati pa mayesero,
Thandizeni Ambuye.
Zonse zam'dziko zidzatha,
Monga Mawu anenera;
Ufumu wanu sudzatha,
Wamuyaya, Wamuyaya.
Palibe wina m'dzikoli,
Ndinutu Mbuyanga;
Pakati pamayesero,
Thandizeni Ambuye.
Nyimbo zonse tidzayimba,
Zanutu Mbuyanga;
Pakati pamayesero,
Thandizeni Ambuye.
