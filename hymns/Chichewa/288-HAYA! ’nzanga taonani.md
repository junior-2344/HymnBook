HAYA! ’nzanga taonani
Yoonekayo
Yakumwamba iti:
“Yesu
Alikudzako.”
Kanganani nkhondo yanu
Ndi mbendera mbuu!
Tibvomere: ‘Inde Mbuye
Mtithangatetu.”
A! mizimu yakuipa
Isautsabe,
Tikaniza zakuipa
Zotizinga ’fe.
Mdani adza nazo mphamvu,
Koma ifetu
Tidzamthetsa nkhondo yake
Timwingitsetu.
Yesu ati, “Ndidza msanga!”
Thangateni phee!
Mbweze mawu akulimba:
Tikanganabe.
