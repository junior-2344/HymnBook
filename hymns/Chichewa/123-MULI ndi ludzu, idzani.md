MULI ndi ludzu, idzani
Kumadzi amoyo
Imwani monse kolere
Kuchitsime konko.
Munka kangati kulaŵa
Madzi onyengera?
Musataye mphamvu yanu,
Musataye chuma.
Imvani, Yesu akuti;
“Chuma ndili nacho,
Landirani, yemwe amva
Akhale ndi moyo.”
Funani Mlungu tsopano.
Akulindirani;
Iye ndiye wachifundo,
Akulandirani.
Ali wokhululukira;
Imvani ananu:
Mtima wake usiyana
Ndi mitima yathu.
Monga m’Mwamba mupambana
Zonse pansi pano;
Choncho maganizo ake
Apitira athu.
Anthu onse a padziko
Adzamvera Mlungu:
Adzakhala ndi chimwemwe,
Nadzamtama ndithu.
