NDIDZATANI ine

NDIDZATANI ine,
Patsiku lomwe;
Ndidzalowa m'dzenje la imfa.
Popeza sindinakonzekere,
Za imfa yomwe ikudzayi.
Konzeka mbale,
Nthawi isanayandikiretu;
Yolowa m'manda,
Momwe sudzamva;
Kuyitana kwake,
M'dzenje la chiyasamire.
Ambuye tipempha,
Mukhale nafe;
Popeza tiri akuchimwa,
Pochita za m'dziko lapansi,
Kuyiwala Mbuye wathuyo.
