MPULUMUTSI Yesu

MPULUMUTSI Yesu,
Mumve nyimbo yathu;
Tikuyamikani
Mumitima mwathu
Zonse tili nazo;
Thupi, mzimu womwe,
Inde, moyo wonse,
Tipereka zonse.
Komwe ndi Chimwemwe
Tidzaimba tonse,
Tidzatama Mbuye
Osalema konse.
Tiyandika, Mbuye,
M’fupifupi ndinu,
Tikugwadirani,
Mutilandire bwino.
Inu munatsika
Kutifera ife;
Inu munakwera
Kuti titsatire.
Haya! Tipitenso
M’njira yomwe ija
Ana a Mulungu
Onse adaponda.
Tizisiya zonse,
Tiziyenda msanga,
Tisamachewuka
Mpaka tipambana.
Tero tidzakwera
Kwawo kwa Mulungu,
Tidzaziiŵala
Zotibvuta ife.
Komwe ndi Chimwemwe
Tidzaimba tonse,
Tidzatama Mbuye
Osalema konse.
