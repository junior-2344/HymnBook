KODI mbale wasimba zachikhululukiro

KODI mbale wasimba zachikhululukiro,
Chomwe ife tikonzekera kumva?
Popachikidwa
Mbuye
Kuwombola 'fe,
Neneranso zachikhululukiro.
Timvere ponenera'we,
Nenera mobwereza;
Mbiri yachipulumutso,
Adzapatsa'we mtendere;
Neneranso zachikhululukiro.
Povutika m'njiramo, waonadi Ambuye,
Alibenso bwenzi lofanana'ye;
Mbuye Yesu anakulandiratuwe, zachikhululukiro.
Kodi walalikira, zambiri yabwinoyo,
Yoposa maloto
Neneranso okometsetsa?
Lalika za Ambuye wathu Yesuyo,
Neneranso zachikhululukiro.
Nkhondo zomwe wamenya
Mpulumutsi wathuyo,
Lalikira zakugonjetsa kwake;
Kudzathandiza iwo okhulupi'ra,
Neneranso zachikhululukiro.
