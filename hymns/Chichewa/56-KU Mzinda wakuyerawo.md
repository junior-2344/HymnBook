KU Mzinda wakuyerawo
Wapachikidwa Mbuyeyo,
Nasautsidwa Mbuyeyo,
Akatipulumutsa ’fe.
Padzanja lachifundolo
Linapenyetsetsa ’khunguwo,
Lachiza nalo nthendazo,
Papyolatu msomaliwo.
Mapazi ake namvatu
Zoŵaŵa zopambanazo,
Napachikidwa kaamba ka
Zoipa zathu zonsezi.
Ambuye, tikondweratu
Kupulumutsa kwanuko;
Satana tisammvere ’yi
Komabe Mpulumutsiyo.
