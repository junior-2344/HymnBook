NDIDZIWITSITSA
Yesu ndi wanga,
Tsiku ndi tsiku ndimakondwera.
Anandigula ndi mwazi wake,
Anandipatsa Mzimu Woyera.
Mawu omwewo, nyimbo yomweyi,
Yesu ndim'tama tsiku lonseli;
Mawu omwewo, nyimbo yomweyi,
Yesu ndim'tama tsiku lonseli.
N'kamvera Yesu ndidzakondwera,
Ndiyang'anitsa nkhope yakeyo;
Ndipo angelo anditengera,
Ine chisomo ndi chuma chake.
Ndine kapolo wa Mbuye wanga,
Andigwiritsa ntchito zakezo;
Ndim'yembekeza adzabweranso,
Kudzanditenga kwawo ndikhale.
