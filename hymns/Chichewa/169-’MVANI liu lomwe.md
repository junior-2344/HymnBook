’MVANI liu lomwe
M’khutu mwakomo,
Alikuitana
Mpulumutsiyo:
“Undipatsetu
Mtima wakowo;
Ndinakuferau
Kalekalelo.”
Ati: “Undiuze
Nsoni zakozo;
Ine ndidzachotsa
Zonse zomwezo.”
Kodi ukafuna
Kupumulatu?
Yesu aitana:
“Udze kunoku.”
Tula mtolo wako
Pali mtandapo,
Akaunyamula
Mpulumutsiyo.
