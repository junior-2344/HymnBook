MASO ndi maso ndi Kristu

MASO ndi maso ndi Kristu,
Kodi ndidzachitanji
M’mene ndidzaona Yesu,
Yemwe anandifera?
'Dzam'ona maso ndi maso
Kuseri kwa mitambo;
Posachedwa ndidzamwona
M’ulemerero wake!
Tsopano ndimwona Iye
Mwa chizimezimetu;
Tsiku lija ndidzaona
Ulemerero wake.
M’mene masautso onse,
Nthenda, imfa, zidzatha,
Dziko likadzakonzedwa,
Ndidzakondwa koposa.
Lodala tsiku lomwelo
Ndidzamwona Yesuyo,
Mpulumutsi wanga yemwe
Anandikonda ine.
