BWENZI lathu ndiye Yesu,
Atikonda ifetu;
Zifunsiro zathu zonse,
Tipemphere Mbuyathu.
Mtima phee tisowa tonse,
Zitivuta chabeko;
Kaamba sitimanka konse,
Kopemphera
Mlunguyo.
Tili nazo zotiyesa,
Zitidetsa nkhawamu;
Tisadandaule chabe,
Tipemphere Mbuyathu.
Kodi tikaona wina,
Tim'khulupiliremo?
Yesu atidziwa bwino,
Tipemphere yekhayo.
Kodi tikulema nazo,
Zotiwawa m'mtimamo?
Atipulumutsa Yesu,
Tipemphere Iyeyu.
Kodi adathawa 'bale?
Pempheratu Bwenzilo:
Akusunga m'mkono mwake,
Udzapumuliramo.
