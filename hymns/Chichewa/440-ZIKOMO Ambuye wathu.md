ZIKOMO Ambuye wathu

ZIKOMO Ambuye wathu,
Tithokoza pakulowa;
M'chaka ichi chatsopano,
Tithokoza kotheratu.
Tithokoza, Inu Mbuye,
Pakulowa m'chakachi;
Tithokoza, Inu Mbuye,
Pakulowa m'chakachi.
Anzathu ena ambiri,
Alephera kuchilowa;
Chaka chatsopano ichi,
Poti munawaitana.
Tipempha Inu Ambuye
Muyambe nafe leroli;
Pakugwira ntchito yanu,
M'chaka ichi chatsopano.
