AMBUYE, thandizenitu

AMBUYE, thandizenitu
Ndi mphamvu zanuzo;
Pantchito zathu zonsezi
Mthandize Mbuyathu.
Zochimwa zathu m’mtimamo
Tilira nazotu;
Mumtima mwathu muti zii;
Mthandize Mbuyathu.
Mthandize Mbuye, timvetu,
Tikalandiredi
Zimene muperekazo
Zakudalitsa ’fe.
Kumwamba Yesu mthandize,
Tilibe wina ’yi:
M’moyo, muimfa mthandize,
Tikhale kwanuko.
