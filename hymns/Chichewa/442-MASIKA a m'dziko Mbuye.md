MASIKA a m'dziko Mbuye

MASIKA a m'dziko Mbuye,
Ayandikira lero;
Potipatsa dzinthu dzathu,
Muzilemekezeka.
Mwatipatsa mvula Mbuye,
Mwatipatsa zokolola,
Mpweya nawo talandira;
Landirani mphatsozi.
Mayiko ena Ambuye,
Mwafikatu chilala;
Anthu ambiri akufa,
Tipempham'wathandize.
M'chikondi chanu Ambuye,
Tikulemekezani;
Mutithangatire ife,
Tikagwire ntchitoyo.
