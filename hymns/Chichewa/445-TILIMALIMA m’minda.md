TILIMALIMA m’minda

TILIMALIMA m’minda,
Tibzala mbewu zathu,
Komatu zibvumbidwa
Ndi Mlunguyo wamphamvu.
Dzuŵalo aŵalitsa
Namatumiza mphepo,
Mbewuzo nameretsa
Mulunguyo wabwinoyo.
Mphatso zonse zathu
Zifuma kwa Mulungu,
Tilemekeze Mbuye wathu,
Atikondadi.
Wolenga ndi Yemweyo
Wa zinthu zonse zathu;
Maudzu ameretsa,
Nayatsanso nyenyezi.
Mphepo zonse zimva,
Nyamazo aziŵeta;
Sadzaiŵala konse
Anake kuŵadyetsa.
Tiyamikira ’Tate,
Zabwino zonse zino,
Kubzala ndi kukunkha
Ndi moyo tili nawo.
Kuninkha Inu Mbuye
Tilibe mtulo wina,
Komatu tipereka
Mitima yoyamika.
