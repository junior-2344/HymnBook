M'MANDA, nagonamo
Yesu Ambuye,
Anadikiratu
Tsiku lija.
M'manda naukamo
Napambana ’'dani akewo;
Anauka kupambana imfayo,
Ali moyo ndi oyeramtimawo.
Wauka, wauka!
Aleluya wauka!
M'manda munalibe
Yesu Ambuye,
Nadikira chabe
Alondawo.
Imfa yalephera
Kumutsekera,
Anachotsa zonse
Zom'zingazo.
