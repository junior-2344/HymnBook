MZIMU ndi mtogoleri
M’njira yachikristuyo,
Atitsogoleratu
Paulendo wathuwu.
M’yoyo ikondweratu
Pomva mawu akewo,
Pakunena Mzimuyo,
“Osokera, bwerani.”
Bwenzi lathu ndinutu,
Mkhale m’fupi nafetu;
Msatisiye tokha ’fe
Pakuyenda m’mdimamu;
Pakuwomba mphepozo
Timaopa m’mtimamo,
Tsono timve mawuwo:
“Amantha ‘nu, bwerani.”
M’mene ntchito yathatu,
M’mene tidikira ’Nu,
Pakukhumba m’Mwambamo,
Tsono tipempherabe.
Nthaŵi ya mabvutowo
Tikhulupirira ’Nu,
Tsono timve mawuwo,
“Okondedwa, bwerani.”
