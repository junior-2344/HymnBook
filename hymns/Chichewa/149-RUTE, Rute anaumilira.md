RUTE, Rute anaumilira

RUTE, Rute anaumilira,
Sindidzakusiyani,
Mapeto ndi imfa.
Ndingakhale ndiyenda,
M'chigwa cha mthunzi wa imfa,
Sindidzawopa konse;
Adzandigwira dzanja.
Poyenda, poyenda,
Poyenda ndi Yesu,
Adzandigwira dzanja Mbuye.
Khama, khama,
khamalo la Rute;
Linam'tengera iye,
Mtendere wosatha.
Yenda, yenda, yenda ndi Yesuyo;
Adzakugwira dzanja,
Ambuye wathuyo.
Mbale, mbale, mbale ulindanji?
Tiyeneradi tonse;
Kukonzekeratu.
Yesu, Yesu, Yesu pakubwera,
Adzatitenga zedi;
Kupita Kumwamba.
