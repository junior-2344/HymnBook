ATI Yesu Mbuyeyo

ATI Yesu Mbuyeyo:
“Ofoka ndinutu,
Mudikire, m’pemphedi,
Ndidzapatsa mphamvutu.”
Yesu wathadi
Za mangaŵatu,
Inde ndinaipa bii!
Watsuka ine mbuu!
Mbuye ndimva leroli
Za mphamvu zanuzi
Zomwe zisandutsadi
Mtima wanga woti bii!
Ine kanthu ndilibe
Kogula moyowu;
Ndingotsuka m’mtimamo
M’mwazi wa Ambuye ’Nu
M’mene ndifa inedi,
Kumwamba mzimuwu
Udzakwera komweko
Kuonana Mbuyeyo.
Ndipo pakuima phe!
Pokhala Yesuyo,
Ndidzamama Mlunguyo
Kosaleka konseko.
