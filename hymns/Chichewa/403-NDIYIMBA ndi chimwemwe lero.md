NDIYIMBA ndi chimwemwe lero

NDIYIMBA ndi chimwemwe lero,
Kale ndidalephera;
Yesu adachotsa machimo.
O! Aleluya!
O! Aleluya-a-a!
O! Aleluya-a-a!
Walowa Yesu mumtima
Mwanga,
O! Aleluya!
Walowa ndi chikondi chake,
Mumtima wachisoni;
Tsiku lonse ndili kuyimba,
O! Aleluya!
Chiyanjano chathu chabwino,
Chiposa china chonse;
Pokhala ndi Yesu tikondwa,
O! Aleluya!
Bwerani ndi nkhawa zanuzo,
Kwa Yesu wokondedwa;
Inunso mudzayimba ndithu,
O! Aleluya!
