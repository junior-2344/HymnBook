ANATILEMBA Yesu tikagwire nkhondo yake

ANATILEMBA Yesu tikagwire nkhondo yake,
Natilimbitsa tipirire osaopa kanthu.
Kwa Satana n’ziwembu zake
Sitigonja konse;
Tiyenderana naye Mfumu yathu yopambana.
Amaliwongo ochuluka amaloŵa m’mtima
Nafuna kuipitsa usana ndi usiku;
Tikatsatabe Mfumu yathu
Sitidzagwa konse,
Pakuti atipatsa mphamvu tipambane nazo.
Amatininkha zida zoteteza mtima,
Ndizo;
Kupempherabe, kukhulupirira ndi kufatsa;
Mtheradi, ndi lupanga ndilo
Mawu a Mulungu;
Pogwira zida zakezi sitilephera konse.
Mafumu atiuza tikaletse ukapolo,
Ndi kumasula goli la anzathu akuopa
Tiyeni, tiŵauze za Mbuyathu Mpulumutsi
Nitiŵanditse mbiri yake
M’dziko lonse lathu.
