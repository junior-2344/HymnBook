M'SANDIPITILIRE Yesu

M'SANDIPITILIRE Yesu,
Mundimvere ine;
Muli kudalitsa ena,
M'sandipitilire.
Yesu Mbuye,
Khutu munditchere;
Mmene muyitana ena,
M'sandipitilire.
Ndigwadira Inu Yesu,
Musandikanize;
Ndikalapatu machimo,
Mbuye, m'ndithandize.
Nkhope yanu ndifunitsa,
Sindiyenera konse;
M'mtima mwanga muchiritse,
Nthenda zanga zonse.
Yesu, ndinutu chitsime,
Cha chimwemwe chokha;
Sindifuna wina konse,
Koma Inu nokha.
