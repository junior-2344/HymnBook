MEMA nkhondoyo, wadza mdaniyo

MEMA nkhondoyo, wadza mdaniyo,
Kweza mbendera ya Mbuye.
Bvala zidazo, imba nyimboyo,
Khazikikatu pa Mulungu.
Haya, anzathu, itilinda nkhondo;
Limbanitu, nkhondo nja Yesu.
Musafoke, m’thyole linga lawo;
Mbuye ndiye Wopambanayo.
Mlungu mverani, mphamvu patseni,
Tithandizeni, tipempha.
Nkhondo itatha, titapambana,
Tidzalandiratu korona.
Mlake mdaniyo, mwimbe m'njiramo
Mpaka nkhondoyo ileka.
Zida konzani, mantha tayani,
Pitikitsani adaniwo.
