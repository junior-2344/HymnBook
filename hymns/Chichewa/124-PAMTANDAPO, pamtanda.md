PAMTANDAPO, pamtanda

PAMTANDAPO, pamtanda,
Yesu ’nafera;
Pamutupo chilemba,
Chisoni, ine.
Analira, “Eloi, Mlungu wanga!
Lama sabakatani,
Mwandisiyiranji?”
Kumandako, kumanda
Yesu nauka;
Anaposa imfayo,
Watha zonsezi.
Ndikondwera, Mbuyanga ’naukadi;
Sekerani nonsenu,
Yesu ngwamoyo.
Pakukwera Kumwamba
Yesu anati,
“Musachoke m’mudzimu,
Pempheranitu;
Mzimu Wakuyeradi adzabwera,
Kuthangata nonsenu,
Kuti msafoke.”
Uzanitu abale,
Onse alongo,
Ndi amitundu yonse
Adzakondwera.
Musaope ntchitoyo yokomadi,
Mphotho yake Kumwamba,
Moyo wosatha.
