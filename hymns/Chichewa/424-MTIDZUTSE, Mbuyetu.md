MTIDZUTSE, Mbuyetu

MTIDZUTSE, Mbuyetu,
Mtionekeremu:
Munene zakukmvekazi,
Timvere ifetu.
Mtidzutse, Mbuyetu,
Mtipatse moyonso;
Mudzetu Mbuye, mudzetu,
Tidalitseni ’fe.
Mtidzutse, Mbuyetu,
Tikweze Dzinali:
Tiuzireni Mpweyawo
M’mitima yathuyi.
Mtidzutse, Mbuyetu,
Timvere Mawudi;
Zoona zanu tonsefe
Tikhulupire ’zi.
Mtidzutse, Mbuyetu,
Mthirenso moyowo;
Ulemu ngwanu nokha ’Nu,
Dalitso nlathulo.
