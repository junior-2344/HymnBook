ATATE, muwapenya
Anzathu ali pano,
Abvomereza Inu
Ambuye Mfumu yawo;
Alonjezetsa lero
Kutsata Mbuye onse
M’ŵathangatire iwo
Asabwerere konse.
Tsopano alandira
Chizindikiro chanu
Munachiika ndinu
Cha Ubatizo wanu
Mulembe dzina lanu
M’mitima mwa onsewa;
M’ŵabatizenso Mbuye,
Ndi Mzimu Wakuyera.
Muŵalimbitse onse
Akhazikike m’mtima,
Akhale chikhalire
Oyera ndi olimba;
Mutithandize tonse
A Mpingo wa Mulungu
Tikhale oyenera
Ndi okondana ndithu.
