KODI mwaona chiyani

KODI mwaona chiyani,
Anzeru a kummawa?
Nyenyezi'yo yaoneka,
Isonyeza njirayo.
Mtendere padziko lonse,
Mtendere padziko lonse;
Kwa anthu onse amene,
Alandira Yesuyo.
Nyenyeziyo pooneka,
Isonyeza chikondi;
Kuti Yesu wabadwadi,
Mu kholalo la ng'ombe.
Ndipo iye wolandira,
Ambuye wathu Yesu;
Akhalanso nawo zedi,
Moyowo wochuluka.
