Lolonjezedwa, Ambuye.
Mnalinena polaŵira
Kwa anyamata, Ambuye.
Kuli chimwemwe m’dzuŵalo
Lakufika Ambuye.
Haa! Ambuye bwerani.
Haa! Ha! Ambuye bwerani.
M’dziko lino chimwemwecho
Chakudza ndi Yesu Mbuye.
Mfumu yathu ya Kumwamba
Ndi dziko lonse lapansi.
Adzakhala pampando
Wakuweruza makamu;
Amitundu adzaima
Pamaso pa Mbuye Yesu.
Adzanena kwa anthuwo
Akudzanjatu lamanja,
“Loŵani ’nu m’chikondwe'ro
Cha Atate nthaŵi zonse.”
