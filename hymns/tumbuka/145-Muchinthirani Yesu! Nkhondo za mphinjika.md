Muchinthirani Yesu! Nkhondo za mphinjika
George James Webb (1803-87)

1 Muchinthirani Yesu! Nkhondo za mphinjika
Tumphurani ndembera; yingatayikanga.
Iyo tiŵarongozge mu kuruska kose;
Ŵarwani tiŵamale: Khristu ndiyo Fumu.
2 Muchinthirani Yesu! Mbata mupulike;
Wukani mukomane mu zuŵa la lero;
Mwimirani mwa ŵanthu; ŵarwani mbanandi;
Wukani na kukhwima, muŵakanizgenge.
3 Muchinthirani Yesu! M’chinthe m’mphavu zake;
Zinu ziti zichepe, m’ngazigomezganga.
Jitorerani homwa, muswenge
Apo zawe lakonda mose muŵengepo.
4 Muchinthirani Yesu! Nkhondo tiyimare,
Nkhondo yiripo sono; na mlenji nkhapota,
Wakuruska tiŵaŵe na wumi wa ndanda,
Themba la uchindani wamkhara nalo swi.
