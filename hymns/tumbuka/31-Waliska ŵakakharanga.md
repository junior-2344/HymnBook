Waliska ŵakakharanga
Este's Psalter, 1592

1 Waliska ŵakakharanga
na mberere zaŵo
Mu dambo la ku Bet’lehemu
nyengo za usiku.
2 He! Buli waka mungelo!
Ungweru nawo tu!
Kuchanya ukafumira
na thenga likuru.
3 “Mwe, mungaŵanga na wofi;
ndizira chimwemwe
Chakusekereska imwe
na mafuko ghose.
4 “Makani ghaweme
ngakuti wababiwira
Munyumba ya Davidi,
Khristu Fumu, Mponoski.
5 “Mujedu wa kuchanya nadi
wasangikeko,
Na saru ŵamdikiska,
wakugona m’lukutu.”
6 Ndipera. Sono ungweru
ukaruska nadi!
Ŵangelo vikwi ŵakamutumbika
Chiuta:
7 “Wachindike Chiuta kuchanya;
na pasi
Ku aŵo wakucha naŵo
paŵenge mutende.”
