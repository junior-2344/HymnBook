Yesu m’pulike, Yesu m’pulike
African Melody

1 Yesu m’pulike, Yesu m’pulike,
Mupulike ta ŵaheni.
2 Mutume Mzimu, Mutume Mzimu.
Watisuke mu mutima.
3 Mutovwirenge, mutovwirenge
Kureka uheni withu.
4 Tina mitima, tina mitima,
Yakuzgoŵera kwananga.
5 Tina makutu, tina makutu.
Ghakujarwa na viheni.
6 Tina mawoko, tina mawoko,
Kweni ghakukhara waka.
7 Taruwa Imwe, taruwa Imwe,
Tareka kukhumba kwinu.
8 Sono zaninge, sono zaninge,
Mutitaske ta ŵaheni.
