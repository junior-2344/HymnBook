Yesu m’bwezi wane m’weme
African Melody

1 Yesu m’bwezi wane m’weme,
Zina linu nda Chimwemwe,
likundisangwiska.
2 Ndap’lika zina liweme,
la Yesu Mponoski wane,
likundisangwiska.
3 Zina liweme chomene
Nadi mu makutu ghane,
likundisangwiska.
4 Zina lakuwombolera
Ŵakwananga wose m’charu,
likundisangwiska
5 Liri kuruska mazina
Na gha ŵangelo kuchanya,
likundisangwiska.
6 Ndilo zina lakutemwa
Likapika m’charu chose,
likundisangwiska.
