Mwomboli withu wandafwe, wakatilayizga
John Bacchus Dykes (1823-76)

1 Mwomboli withu wandafwe, wakatilayizga
Kutininkha Msanguruski kuŵa nase.
2 Wakiza m’dimi la moto kusambizga ŵanthu;
Nga ndi mphepo yikuputa mu udesi.
3 Wakiza kapa chimango, mbali wa uchizi,
Wakukharamo mu mtima wakuzika.
4 Mazgu ghake tapulika, ghakunozga nadi;
Ghakukanizga uheni na chofyoso.
5 Mphavu izo tiri nazo, na kutonda kose,
Na makhumbiro ghaweme, nvyake pera.
6 Mzimu wakutowa nadi, muŵe na uchizi;
Muzgore mitima yithu nyumba yinu.
7 M’tumbike Dada na Mwana na Mzimu Mutuŵa;
Chiuta withu yumoza mu utatu.
