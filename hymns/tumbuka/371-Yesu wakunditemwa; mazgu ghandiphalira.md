Yesu wakunditemwa; mazgu ghandiphalira
William Batchelder Bradbury (1816-68)

1 Yesu wakunditemwa; mazgu ghandiphalira;
Twana tuchoko nthwake, ndi murara wa tose.
E! Wakutemwa! Wakunditemwa!
E! wakutemwa! Mazgu ghakuphara.
2 Yesu wakandifwira; wakajura kuchanya,
Wandisuke uheni; kuchanya nkhanjirenge.
3 Yesu wakurongozga; wali pafupi nane;
Tiwatorenge ine, kuya nane kuchanya.
