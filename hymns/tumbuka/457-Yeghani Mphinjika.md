      1. Yeghani mphinjika yinu, ndimo Yesu wanenera
         Pakurondezga Chiuta, mujiyuyure mumtima.

      2. Yeghani mphinjika yinu, kopa chara mufiskenge
         nkhongono zake Chiuta ndizo ziŵenge pa imwe.

      3. Yeghani mphinjika yinu rekani kuchita soni
         Yesu wakazizipizga, kufwa mumalo mwa ise.

      4. Yeghani mphinjika yinu, mwendenge mchisisi chake
         mudindi mwamkufumamo kuya ku kaya la ndanda.

      5. Yeghani mphinjika yinu rondezgani khristu Yesu
         Mphumphu yakutowa nadi yikumrindizgani imwe.
