Nkhwenda m’kudoka msumba Zioni
kuchanya | African Melody

1 Nkhwenda m’kudoka msumba Zioni kuchanya
A laŵiska pasirya mbanjani ŵakwimba?
Ndi aŵo ŵakendanga na Fumu.
2 Nadi-nadi nkudoka kwendamo mu nthowa
Yakuya ku muzi uweme ndi Zioni,
Kukondwera pamoza na iŵo.
3 Pakwendamo mu nthowa, vitima, urwani
Na visuzgo, urwirwi, masozi na nyifwa,
Kweni Yesu wakoma zimbozga.
4 Nanga vindiwukire viyezgo mu nthowa,
Nkhugomezga Fumu kundisungirira’mwe,
Ndisuke nkawonane na Fumu.
