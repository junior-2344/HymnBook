Ŵadada Chiuta
African Melody

1 Ŵadada Chiuta
tikujisenderezgaso
Kwa ku Imwe mwa ŵatuŵa.
2 Tananga, tajumpha pa
marango agho
Imwe M’li kutirongora kale.
3 Tapusa tarondezga
makhumbo gha ku nyifwa,
Tasunjizga khumbo linu.
4 Tavuka, tavuka,
na kupwatapwata m’thondo
Mwambura na chipumulo.
5 Chiuta m’tiwuske,
tiŵe ŵakufikapo nadi
Apo mukutikhumbira.
