Nanga visuzgo vikulimbana
African Melody

1 Nanga visuzgo vikulimbana,
Nanga virwani vikundizira, ‘Vyo vyachi!
Ine nkhutenthema
chara Yehova ndiyo ndi movwiri wane.
2 Viyuni m’dera wakuvirera,
Ndivyo viŵenge nga
nchikumbusko;
Ŵateŵeti ŵake ntha ŵam’kusokwa;
Yehova ndiyo ndi Movwiri waŵo.
3 Uko tikwenda ntha tikumanya,
Tikupulikira nchemerezgo,
Chirongozgi withu ntha wakusoŵa,
Yehova ndiyo ndi movwiri withu.
4 Taŵakwananga tikumrondezga,
Mthaski wachema, ndi Yesu Fumu;
Ndiyo linga lithu lakuphokwera,
Yehova ndiyo ndi movwiri withu.
