Njani wali kumo na Fumu Yesu?
Adapted by John Goss (1800-80)

1 Njani wali kumo na Fumu Yesu?
Njani wayovwire kutaska ŵanthu?
Njani tiwareke vinthu vya charu
Kurwa na murwani m’zina la Mwene?
Mu lusungu lwinu muli kuchema,
Tiri namwe kumo,
Yesu Mponoski.
2 Yesu mwatigula na chuma chara;
Ndopa zinu ndizo zatiwombora;
Uyowizakwinu mukum’tumbika
Na wawanagwa winu na Mwamphu wuwo,
Na uchizgi winu Mwatiwombola,
Tiri namwe kumo,
Yesu Mponoski.
3 Zawe lingakonda, murwani wime,
Ntha wangavitonda vita vya Themba,
Tichinthe nganga nga, mpingo wa Yesu,
Unenesko wake utirusizge,
Na Chimwemwe chose tikalembeka,
Tiri namwe kumo, Yesu Mponoski.
4 Tiri kusoreka m’charu chiyeni;
Ta vita vya Fumu tigomezgeke;
M’mlimo wa ufumu tingadondanga,
Tiŵe na chikanga na ŵakuziza,
Na uchizi winu m’tisungirire,
Tiŵe winu Yesu mazuwa ghose.
