Wankhondo ŵa mphinjika jitorerani uta
Medieval French Melody

1 Wankhondo ŵa mphinjika jitorerani uta;
Ŵarwani mba nkhongono, m’pote homwa, muchinthe.
2 Pa charu cha uheni tumphuskani ndembera;
Mureke yikhupuke, muyiyeghe mwinuske.
3 M’vikaya vya ŵakavu na ŵambura kumanya
Thenga la Muponoski liphare mazgu ghake.
4 Uko kuli mudima m’yeghereko marazi;
Uko kuli uheni m’rongore chiphokwero.
5 Ku awo mbakavu m’nenere chiphokwero;
Vichwapi na ŵa soni m’yowoyere vya nchindi.
6 M’sungirire ŵaranda, m’sanguruske ŵa suzgo,
M’nkhongono za Chiuta muparanye uheni.
7 Ndembera yikhupuke, lipanga mukorere,
Vyaru vyose visuke vozgoke vya Chiuta.
