Ŵada mutumbike mulimo uwo tachita
Rev. J.P. Chirwa

1 Ŵada mutumbike mulimo uwo tachita,
Mulaŵiske ŵose aŵo
ŵapulika mazgu ghinu.
2 Mutumbike mazgu ghinu, ghapambike visoro;
Ndimwe mwekha mukumezga
mbuto yinu ya umoyo.
3 A Mzimu muguzire ŵose mu nthowa yiweme;
Aŵo mbakudonda mtima
musambizge wino-wino.
4 A Chiuta withu yumo, muŵapenge uchizi;
Ŵachitenge vinthu vyose
viyane na khumbo linu.
