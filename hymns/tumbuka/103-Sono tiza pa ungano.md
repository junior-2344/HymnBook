Sono tiza pa ungano
P. Banda

1 Sono tiza pa ungano,
Zani, zaninge,
Fumu mutitumbike.
2 Dada withu wa kuchanya,
Khani, Khaninge Themba,
mutovwirenge.
3 Tampukwani Imwe pera,
Dada, Dada wa wezi
mutiphotore.
4 Tikulira kwa ku Imwe,
Yesu mutipulike ise ŵaheni.
5 Mutisavye mu mitima,
Yesu tikumanenge na Imwe sono.
