Yesu, chimwemwe cha mtima
Henry Percy Smith (1825-98)

1 Yesu, chimwemwe cha mtima,
’Mwe lumuli lwa taŵanthu,
Vyam’charu ntha vikukhuska,
Vyinu ndivyo tikukhumba.
2 Nenesko linu likwima,
’Mwe Mtaski wa ŵakulira;
Awo ŵakumusangani
Ŵakuti, mukupa vyose.
3 Ndimwe chibama, tikurya;
Tamba kale, tiryengeso;
Ndimwe chisimi, tikumwa,
Mizimu yithu yimweso.
4 Taŵa mayinga tadoka,
Mutinjizge m’chipumulo;
Tichenge pakumuwonani,
Timkorani na chimwemwe.
5 A Yesu mukhare nase,
Mutipe ungweru winu
Mdima umare, tiwone
Kungweruka kwinu, Fumu.
