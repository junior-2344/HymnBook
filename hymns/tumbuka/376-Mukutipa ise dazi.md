Mukutipa ise dazi
Arr. E. Rawdon Bailey

1 Mukutipa ise dazi,
tikumwongani Dada;
Mwezi na nyenyezi zose,
tikumwongani Dada;
Maluŵa ghose ghaweme
agho ghakumera m’munda,
Na tuyuni twa mudera,
tikumwongani Dada.
2 Mukutipaso makuni,
tikumwongani Dada;
Mphepo na vura yiweme,
tikumwongani Dada;
Na vyakurya vose vyithu
mukutilera nadi swi,
Mukutipwerera nadi,
tikumwongani Dada.
3 Mukatipa Yesu Khristu,
tikumwongani Dada,
Wakiza muno mu charu,
tikumwongani Dada;
Wakafwira ta ŵanthu ndiyo
Muponoski withu
Uyo wakutitemwa swi,
tikumwongani Dada.
