Chiuta ndi linga
African Melody | Salmo 46

1 Chiuta ndi linga,
Movwiri mu suzgo,
Nanga charu chisezgeke,
ise ntha titope.
2 Mapiri ghangawa m’nyanja,
maji ghangapopoma,
Inya, ghangasunkhunika na
jigha likuru.
3 Muronga ukusangwiska
m’sumba wa Chiuta,
Malo uko mukukhara,
Fumu yikuru ’mwe,
4 Chiuta wali mwa uwo,
ntha tiusezgeke;
Sekuru tiwawovwire luŵiro
luŵiro.
5 Mafuko ghakacheruka,
na maufumuso;
Chiuta wakayowoya:
charu chatenthema.
6 Fumu ya vita yichali
kukhara nase swi;
Leza wa Yakobe ngwithu,
ndiyo mphanji yithu.
