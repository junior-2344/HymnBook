Kuli chisopo chenecho Chiuta ntha
wakukana | H.J. Gauntlett

1 Kuli chisopo chenecho Chiuta ntha wakukana,
Ndicho nchakuzirwa – mtima wakupera.
2 Uyo ŵakumsindamira ŵakuchanya, na kumsopa,
Tiwakhareso mu mtima wakupera.
3 Mtuŵa, Mwana wa Chiuta, iyo tiwagowokere,
Wajipatulire mtima wakupera.
4 Mzimu Mtuŵa, Msanguruski, tiwapulike kulira,
Wati watozge mutima wakupera.
5 Muponoski nkhugomezga; ndikozgane na mwa ŵene;
Mu lusungu m’ndipe mtima wakupera.
