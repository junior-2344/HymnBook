ADDITIONAL LITURGICAL TEXTS

In 2014 the Synod Assembly approved a revised order for the morning service. This
service included revised versions of the Apostles’ Creed and the Lord’s Prayer. We
hope that these revised versions will eventually find their way into the “Hymns for
Malawi”. Until that time, in order to promote use of these revised versions, we
include them in this edition of the Sumu za Sono.
