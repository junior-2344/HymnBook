Muponoski wakuchema wose
African Melody

1 Muponoski wakuchema wose,
Iyo ndiyo wakuchema ise.
2 Fumu Yesu tikumurombani
Mutume Mzimu wawuske ise.
3 Imwe ŵanthu muzgokenge mose,
Wakuchema Mponoski wa charu.
4 Wakizira ku ponoska ŵanthu;
Mponoski ndiyo Yesu yekha.
5 Pulikani imwe ŵakwananga
Wakuchema, “Zaninge kwa ine.”
