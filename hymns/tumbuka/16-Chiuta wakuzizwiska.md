Chiuta wakuzizwiska
Ravenscroft's Psalter, 1621

1 Chiuta wakuzizwiska
Kuchita minthondwe;
Pa bingu wakukwerera,
Wakwenda pa maji.
2 Khumbo lake lakutowa
Wakufiska pera;
Mu vinjeru na m’kutemwa
Vyose wakuchita.
3 Sangwani mwa ’kutenthema:
Ghazura lusungu
Mabingu agho mukopa
Ghapenge thumbiko
4 Ungeruzgira Chiuta
Pa vyakumanyikwa;
Gomezga lusungu lwake,
Ndiyo wakutemwa
5 Apo vikwiza visuzgo
Wachali kutemwa;
Mtima wake ngwa lusungu,
Nanga wakulanga
6 Ŵambura kupulikana
Mbakusokwa nadi;
Ŵakumtemwa ŵakumanya
Kurunjika kwake.
