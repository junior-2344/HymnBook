Chiuta wakulawiska tuyuni twa mdera
S.W. Straus

1 Chiuta wakulawiska tuyuni twa mdera,
Usange wakututemwa nane wanditemwa.
Wakutemwa, wakutemwa, wakutemwa ine;
Wakutemwa tunthu tose, wakutemwa ine.
2 Maluŵa ghose gha m’thondo ndiyo waghalenga,
Usange wakughatemwa, wakutemwa ine.
3 Tuyuni, maluŵa, vyose Chiuta walenga,
Ntha tiwaruwenge twana. Wakutemwa tose.
