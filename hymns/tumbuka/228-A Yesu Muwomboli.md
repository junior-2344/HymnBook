A Yesu Muwomboli
E. Chavura

1 A Yesu Muwomboli,
zani muchizge ine;
Lero mundiponoske
ku zakwananga;
Nkhumŵeyani A Khristu
ndiŵenge munthu winu.
2 Wuskanimo uheni,
mundisuke na ndopa;
Ndopa zinu, A Yesu,
zindisukiske;
Mtima wane munozge;
munthu winu ndiŵenge.
3 Ine vyalema lero,
uheni wandipyora;
Ndizenge ku Kalvari,
uko m’kafwira;
Nkhawutaye uheni
pa Imwe Muponoski.
4 Nkhujipereka sono,
ndiri winu ndipera;
M’ndikanizge, A Yesu
ndingananganga;
Muzga winu ndiŵenge;
ndi m’teŵeteraninge.
5 Sono ine ndazgoka;
Yesu wandiwombola,
Ndiri mwanangwa lero;
ndawomboleka,
Kuwuzga wa satana,
uyo wakandikora.
