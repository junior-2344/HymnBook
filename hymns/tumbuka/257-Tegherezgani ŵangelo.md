Tegherezgani ŵangelo
Anon.

1 Tegherezgani ŵangelo,
ŵakuwuska sumu,
Wakwimbira Fumu yithu,
Fumu ya ’chindami,
Pulikizgani munthondwe
uwo ŵakwimbira,
Wachindike Mponoski,
ndiyo wakuwusa.
2 Mutende uŵenge m’charu
na vvakukharamo,
Aŵo mbakusutulika ndipo
ŵasoreka,
Ŵakwimba mazgu ghakuti,
Muponoski wiza,
Charu cha pasi na mtambo,
mpokerani Yesu.
3 Mwa ŵanthu koreskaninge,
mumanyiske zina,
Lilembeke pa mutima, muti
mukondwenge,
Koreskaninge makani
gha kubabika kwake,
Yegherani kose-kose makani gha mtende.
