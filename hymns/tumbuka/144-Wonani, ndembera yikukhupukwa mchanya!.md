Wonani, ndembera yikukhupukwa mchanya!
P.P. Bliss

1 Wonani, ndembera yikukhupukwa mchanya!
Zavumbukwa nkhongono za kukhomezezga.
“Khozgani linga mwa ŵanthu, Yesu wachema;
Wezgerani mazgu ghithu, “Fumu tikwima.”
2 Satana wakurotoka na vita vyakhe:
Vinkhara vyithu vikuwa: tafipa m’tima.
3 Chinthani, mwa ŵanthu, ise tina nkhongono
Kulimbana na Satana murwani withu.
4 Zawe nidnonono nadi kweni kwafika
Uyo ndi Mrongozgi withu; timuhenere!
