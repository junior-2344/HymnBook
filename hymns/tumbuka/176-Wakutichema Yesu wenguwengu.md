Wakutichema Yesu wenguwengu
Will L. Thompson

1 Wakutichema Yesu wenguwengu,
Tose, iwe na ine;
Wakutilindilira na kuzika,
Wakutichema tose.
Zani, zaninge, ‘mwe mukuwuka zani,
Nadi Yesu wakuchema ta ŵanthu
Kuti, Zaninge kwane.
2 Mwachedwerachi?
Yesu wakuŵeya,
Wakuŵeyera ise;
Mwakanirachi lusungu lukuru
Ulo wakurongora?
3 Mazuŵa ghithu ghakulutirira,
Ghakuruta muyaya.
Tose tikusendelera ku nyifwa,
Na yumo wangaphokwa.
4 Okwe! Kutemwa kwa Yesu nkhukuru,
Umo wakutemwera;
Nanga tachimwa wakugowokera,
Wakutigowokera.
