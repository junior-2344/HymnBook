Tumbikani Yehova, Aleluya
Robert Williams (1781-1821)

1 Tumbikani Yehova, Aleluya;
’Mwe ŵa m’charu cha pasi,
Aleluya;
’Mwe ŵangelo kuchanya,
Aleluya;
Muli m’kutemwa kwake,
Aleluya.
2 Vumbuliranani ’mwe
Zina lake likuru,
Nthanga yose ya m’charu
Yimchindike ruta swi.
3 Thuwuskani lusungu,
Rumbani wezi wake,
Uwo watirongora,
Uwo wiza na Yesu.
4 Bango, mazgu, mitima,
Vikoŵerane m’sumu,
Vyamoyo vyose vimbe,
Vimchindike ruta swi.
