Kutemwa kwakuruska mapimiko
Joseph Francis Thrupp (1827-67)

1 Kutemwa kwakuruska mapimiko,
Ta ŵa pasi tikumlambirani,
Nakuweyerera ŵaŵiri aŵa,
Ŵaŵiri aŵo ŵazgoka yumo.
2 Imwe mkaŵa munthu wakufikapo,
M’ŵape chifundu na kuthulana;
M’wape chizizipizgo chakuzika,
Na chigomezgo chambura kopa.
3 M’wape Chimwemwe chakuŵangweruska,
M’ŵape mutende wakutuniska;
Na pa kumara lwendo pano pasi
M’ŵape umoyo wa myirayira.
