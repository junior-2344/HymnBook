Tiyeni, ŵaKhristu, tipote homwa
Arthur Seymour Sullivan (1842-1900)

1 Tiyeni, ŵaKhristu, tipote homwa,
Yesu Nkhoswe yithu wakurongozga;
Fumu ya kuchanya yatinenera
Na ndembera zake zikudangira.
Ŵakhristu, tiyeni, tipote homwa,
Yesu Nkhoswe yithu wakurongozga.
2 Aŵo mba Satana ŵakuchimbira,
Mpingo wa ŵakhristu ukuŵaruska;
Satana wakopa para tikwimba,
Koreskani sumu, mwa’bali ŵithu.
3 Ngati ndi mupingo tende ŵakhristu,
Uko ŵakendako ŵose ŵatuŵa;
Tingapatukana, tilinganenge,
M’chigomezgo, m’luso, na m’chitemwano.
4 Mafumu ghakufwa na maufumu,
Ufumu wa khristu ngwa m’yirayira;
Aŵo mba Satana mphavu zawo go!
Yesu wapangana ndiyo wamvivya.
5 Zaninge, mwa ŵanthu, tiŵe pamoza,
Kowerani nase Sumu ya Khristu;
Warumbike Yesu, Karonga withu;
Mu miwiro yose tose timbenge.
