’We mayinga pulikiska, takwizira na mazgu
Hubert P. Main

1 ’We mayinga pulikiska, takwizira na mazgu;
Yesu wanozga monesko watikupokerere.
Reka mpandira wa waka, Yesu wakukuchema;
Iza, vyose vyanozgeka; kasi ukulindachi?
2 Ka uheni wazotofya? Wize kawuzomera:
Wati wakugowokere; romba ’we kulinda cha!
3 Mu mawoko gha kuYesu ndimo utheghamenge;
Wakusavye pachiziwa; ’we izanga luŵiro.
4 Wona maraya gha nthengwa wakuvwarika sono;
’we pusumpha ku vyakurwa; njira, ukulindachi?
