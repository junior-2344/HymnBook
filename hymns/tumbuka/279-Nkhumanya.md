Nkhumanya
Ira D. Sankey

1 Nkhumanya
m’kundichinthiska,
Muponoski wane;
Mose umo mwakanda ’mwe
ndimo nkhurondezga.
Pakupulikana namwe ndiri na thumbiko;
Ndimwe nyanguruya ine, chigomezgo chane.
2 Nkhumanya
m’kundichinthiska
m’wofi na m’urwani
Uwo ungandipuruska
na kunditenthemya.
3 Nkhumanya
m’kundichinthiska,
m’kundisungirira,
Nanga ndende mu chindimba,
na mu chisi wuwo.
4 Nkhumanya
m’kundichinthiska,
mutindipasizge;
Uchizi winu ngukuru
na ku umariro.
