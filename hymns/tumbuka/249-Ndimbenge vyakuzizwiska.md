Ndimbenge vyakuzizwiska
Rowland Hugh Prichard (1811-87)

1 Ndimbenge vyakuzizwiska
vya Muwomboli wane;
Wakasida uchindami,
wakatora Kalvari.
Ndimbenge vyakuzizwiska vya
Muwomboli wane;
Ndimbe pamo na ŵatuwa pa muronga wa moyo.
2 Nkhapurukira m’thengere;
wakiza kandipenja;
Kukaya wateteskera;
sono ndiri mu nthowa.
3 Ine nkhafwanga lunkhuwa,
nkhateremuka ruta,
Chigomezgo nkhachitaya;
kutemwa kwandiwezga.
4 Nanga nthowa yende m’mdima,
nanga chize chitima,
Yesu ruta wali nane,
Iyo wakundikhozga.
5 Yesu wati wende nane
ndisuke nkhafikeko
Ku chikhazi cha ŵadada,
nda wakupotoreka.
