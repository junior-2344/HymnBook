A Chiuta mu uchizi winu
African Melody

1 A Chiuta mu uchizi winu
Ndiza sono panthazi pinu;
M’zina la Yesu Mponoski wane,
Mundithutire mvuchi wa moyo.
Mundithutire mvuchi wamoyo wa muyirayira,
Mundithutire mvuchi
wa chikosa mu milimoyinu.
2 Uchindere wane wandizgora
Chikozi cha umoyo wane;
Ndapulikira ivyo nvya nyifwa,
M’ndithutire mvuchi
wa mahara.
3 Nkhutondeka kumteŵeterani;
Nkhujipa mundizgore sono,
Lero ndiwe muteŵeti winu,
Mundithutire mvuchi
wa pamphu.
4 Mahara ghane ghakuwukira
Ku vyakuvunda na ku nyifwa;
Sono mudichizge kose-kose,
Ndipenjenge vya umoyo nadi.
