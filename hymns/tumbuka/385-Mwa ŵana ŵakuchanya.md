Mwa ŵana ŵakuchanya
School Hymn

1 Mwa ŵana ŵakuchanya
uku m’kwenda mwimbenge;
Mwimbirenge Mponoski,
wakuruska m’utuwa.
Lwendo kuya kuchanya
ŵakendamo ŵadada;
Mbakucha sono iŵo,
tamwona kucha kwaŵo.
2 M’samphuske, mwa, ’thumbiko;
mwamkukhara na Yesu,
Malo wamnozgerani,
mwayamkuŵa nayo swi,
Ŵana ŵa kungweruka
laŵiskani musumba;
Kaya nja ndanda nadi;
Fumu tamkuyiwona.
3 Mungopanga, chinthani,
m’mphaka za charu chinu;
Khristu Mukuru winu wakuti,
“nthokoniko.”
Fumu tikuzomera;
vyose tikuvireka;
Muŵenge Chirongozi,
titimrondezganinge.
