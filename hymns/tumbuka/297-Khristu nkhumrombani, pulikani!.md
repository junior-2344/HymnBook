Khristu nkhumrombani, pulikani!
L.G. Hayne

1 Khristu nkhumrombani, pulikani!
Mundisambizgenge kumtemwani;
Khristu, nkhumŵeyani,
m’ndovwire kutemwa – kumtemwani.
2 Kale nkhapenjanga makondwero:
Lero nkhuti, “Imwe m’ndisorere”
Nkhumrombani, Khristu,
m’ndovwire kutemwa – kumtemwani,
3 Chitima chovwire, na kuŵinya,
Vingandipamphuska kumtemwani,
Navyo vimbe nane
kuti ndimtemwani, ndimtemwani.
4 Marumbo ndimbenge apo nkhufwa,
ndichali kuromba ichi pera;
kuti ndimtemwani
na mutima wose – ndimtemwani.
