Yesu Mponoski wane wakiza
E.E. Hasty (arr.)

1 Yesu Mponoski wane wakiza,
Wakagona m’uryero wa ng’ombe;
Nadi nchakuzukuma chomene,
Wakapenja ine.
Wakapenja ine, wakapenja ine;
Nadi nchakuzukuma chomene;
wakapenja ine.
2 Yesu wakafuta m’randu wane,
Pa Kalvari wakandiwombola;
Nadi nchakuzukuma chomene,
Wakafwira ine.
Wakafwira ine, wakafwira ine;
Nadi nchakuzukuma chomene,
wakafwira ine.
3 Yesu Mponoski, wakandipenja
Apa nkhapulukira kuwaro,
Wakapepeka uzima wane;
Wakachema ine.
Wakachema ine, wakachema ine;
Nadi nchakuzukuma chomene,
wakachema ine.
4 Yesu wati wikhenge kuchanya –
Ghakunozga mapangano ghake;
Inya ndazamkumwona wakwikha;
Wakwizira ine.
Wakwizira ine, wakwizira ine;
Nadi nchakuzukuma chomene,
wakwizira ine.
