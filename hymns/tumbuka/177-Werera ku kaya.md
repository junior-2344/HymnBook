Werera ku kaya
W.H. Doane

1 Werera ku kaya,
vyalema mu m’tima,
Nthowa nja mudima,
ndipo nja mayiyi.
Mwana wakusoŵa,
wera, wera, wera,
Wera, wera, sono.
2 Werera ku kaya wakulindilira
Pa muryango mu m’dima
Yesu wakwimirira.
3 Werako, zgokako ku viheni vyako,
Vyakusuzga vyako na vitima vyose.
4 Werera, werera, wakukhumba iwe,
Wakugowokera na kukupokerera.
