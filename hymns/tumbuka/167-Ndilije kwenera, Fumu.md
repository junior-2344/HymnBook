Ndilije kwenera, Fumu
William Hurst (1849-1934)

1 Ndilije kwenera, Fumu,
kuti mwize kwane;
’Mwa ’tuŵa ndimwe
A Yesu, nda muheni ndine.
2 Muyowoye mazgu pera,
mazgu gha lusungu,
Ghangasutula muheni
ku viheni vyake.
3 Ndilije kwenera,
apo muzimu nguheni,
Usange mukunjiramo
mungachizga ine.
4 Ndilije kwenera, kweni
nkhukhumbiska Imwe,
Mukathira ndopa zinu
kuponoska ine.
5 A Yesu zaninge sono,
mundikhutiskenge,
Muzuzgeso na kutemea
mtima wa chikwangu.
