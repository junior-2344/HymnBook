Dada m’titumbike, muŵe na lusungu
Robert Archibald Smith (1780-1829) | Salmo 67

1 Dada m’titumbike, muŵe na lusungu;
Mafuko ghose gha m’charu gham’manyani Imwe.
2 Wose pano pasi ŵamrumbani Imwe,
Mafuko ghasekerere na kwimba zisumu.
3 Mwamkweruzga ŵanthu na urunji winu,
Mwamkuŵarongozga Fumu, na kutemwa kwinu.
4 Ŵam’rumbani Imwe na kukondwa kose,
Ŵarara na ŵana ŵimbe na kumuwongani.
5 Charu chapambika vipambi viweme,
Chiuta wamkutumizga vitumbiko vyakhe.
6 Wamkutitumbika mu mazuŵa ghose,
Ku magomero gha charu ŵanthu ŵamchindike.
