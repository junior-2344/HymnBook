Wakongana yumo-yumo
S.& S. 574 old

1 Wakongana yumo-yumo
mu charu cha murere;
Ŵakundonda mu mipingo
kuya m’charu chiweme.
Mba muyirayira sono, pano kuwonana cha;
Tamkumana nawo ŵose mwene Iwendo Iwamara.
2 Ŵakuruta wengu-wengu,
ŵakuzgeŵerekera;
Ŵana ŵithu na ŵabali aŵo
tikuŵatemwa.
3 Kweni aŵo ŵakutemwa Fumu
yithu Mponoski
Ŵamkukumanaso mula
m’nyumba zakunozgeka.
