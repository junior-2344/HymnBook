Chemerezga iwe wasuzgika na uheni wako
African Melody

1 Chemerezga iwe wasuzgika na uheni wako;
Yesu tiwakupulike, ndipo tiwakuchizgenge.
Chemerezga, chemerezga
kwa Yesu Mponoski;
Iyo ngwa lusungu
tiwakuchizgenge.
2 Wapulikizga sono Yesu wakukufumba iwe,
Ute, ute na kumzgora, Iyo tiwakuchizgenge.
3 Ungatukumizganga para uheni ukuchontha;
Ute, ute na kumchema, Iyo wakuchizgenge.
4 Yesu ndi ng’anga yenecho yakufikapo nkhanira;
Munyakhe kulive uyo wangakuponoska nadi.
