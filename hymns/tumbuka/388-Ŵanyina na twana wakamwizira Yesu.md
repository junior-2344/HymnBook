Ŵanyina na twana wakamwizira Yesu
H. & S.H. 64

1 Ŵanyina na twana wakamwizira Yesu,
Ndipo ŵasambiri wakawakanizganga;
Kweni Yesu wakawona, wakawaso na lusungu;
“Rekani twana twize kwa Ine.”
2 Nditupokerere ndipo nditupakate;
’Mwe nditiŵe muliska wa twana tutema;
Uvwe tukunditemweska twamkhara nane kuchanya,
“Rekani twana twize kwa Ine.”
3 Mponoski wakalengera lusungu twana;
Tunandi tundapulike vya zina lake
Tundaŵazge buku lake, na mazgu agho wakati,
“Rekani twana twize kwa Ine.”
4 Fumu tikuromba kuti mafuko ghose
Ghafiske mazgu, ghataye ŵangoza ŵawo;
M’ŵare pa ito ŵadada, kujiwonya ŵakutemwa,
M’sambizge twana twize kwa Imwe.
