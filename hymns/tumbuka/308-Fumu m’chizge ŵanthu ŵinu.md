Fumu m’chizge ŵanthu ŵinu
African Melody

1 Fumu m’chizge ŵanthu ŵinu;
Tina zakwananga ise;
Tikulira na uheni withu,
Fumu m’chizge ŵanthu ŵinu wose.
2 Fumu m’vwire ŵanthu ŵinu;
Tikupenja Imwe lero;
Tikumara na uheni withu,
Fumu tikuzomerezga Imwe.
3 Fumu m’gowokere ise;
Tiza sono kwa ku Imwe;
Tazomera zakwananga zithu;
Fumu m’gowokere ise tose.
4 Ndimwe Dada withu m’kuru;
Mukatuma Mwana winu,
Wakafwira ta ŵanthu ŵaheni;
Fumu tikuzomerezga Imwe.
