Nkhusekera pa kupulika
African Melody

1 Nkhusekera pa kupulika
Kuti tiye ku nyumba
ya ku Chiuta.
2 M’nyumba ya ku Chiuta wane,
Ndimo muli uzengi
wa Mzimu wane.
3 Dada Chiuta Mwati wane
Zani mwate m’tima upya
mwa ku ine.
4 Mtima wane ndam’jurirani
Zani m’njire yiŵe nyumba
yinu, Dada.
5 Mundovwire kumusopani
Mu muzimu na m’nenesko
nyengo zose.
6 Para nkhuwerera ku kaya
Ndiyeghemo Imwe mu mu
mutima wane.
