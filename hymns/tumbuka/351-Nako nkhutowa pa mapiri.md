Nako nkhutowa pa mapiri
R.A. Smith | Yesaya 52 v. 7 | Nako nkhutowa pa mapiri, | Malundi gha uyo wakwiza | na mtende, (Kaŵiri) | Wakuthuwuska mtende mwenecho, | Wiza na uthenga, | ’thenga uweme; | Wakwenda na mphonokwa | kapharira ŵanthu | Leza withu ndiyo Themba | Pinganinge ’mwe! | Mwimbe mose, mwimbe mose | Mwa ŵanthu muli na chitima; | Nadi Leza wali kusangwiska | na kuponoska ŵanthu ŵake | Aleluya, Aleluya, mum’chindike | Aleluya, Aleluya, Fumu yithu.

1. MWAMVA Mawu a Mulungu; 
Mukabvomeranjiko? 
Kodi musandula mtima 
Ndi kumvera Yesuyo? 
Bvomerani, Bvomerani 
Bvomerani msangatu, 
Musazengereze ayi, 
Landirani Yesuyo. 
2. Munachita nthantha kale, 
Musazengereze ’yi; 
Yesu akulindirani, 
Sadzakana inu ’yi. 
3. Musaope pokumbuka 
Zakuipa anuzo; 
Khulupilirani Yesu, 
Adzachotsa zonsezo 
4. Perekani mtima wanu 
Kwa Mulungu wathuyo; 
Khulupirirani Yesu
