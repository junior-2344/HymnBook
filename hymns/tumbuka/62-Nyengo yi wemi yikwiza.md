Nyengo yi wemi yikwiza
H & H.S. 351

1 Nyengo yi wemi yikwiza,
ivangeli lam’konda;
Mafuko ghose gha pasi
ghapulika mphomezi;
Vinkhwawo vyam’tumphuskika,
na mapiri ghamsangwa
Nyanja zam’wuska marumbo,
zam’koŵerana kwimba.
Matanda-kucha ghakwiza zuwa laneng’enera,
Ndi nyengo ya vitumbiko, tihenerenge tose.
2 Nyengo yiweme yikwiza,
nkhongono ziti zimare,
Ndipo mazgu gha Mponoski
gham’pereka chimango.
Ubali wam’kuwoneka,
wofi uti umare;
Ŵarwani nawo kwam’ŵavye,
ŵatuŵa ŵam’pumula.
3 Nyengo yiweme yikwiza,
yikapharika kale;
Yikulayizgika m’mazgu,
ŵatuŵa ŵakaloska;
Awo ŵakukhara m’chisi ŵati ŵangweruskike
Ŵa m’charu ŵarutirire,
kuwombokwa kwafika.
