Dada withu wakulinda ise
African Melody

1 Dada withu wakulinda ise,
Kutovwira na kutipa uweme,
Na kutipa uweme.
2 Mungopanga, mulawiske Dada,
Wakovwira ŵakuvuka luŵiro;
Wakovwira luŵiro.
3 Laŵiskani na tuyuni, twana;
Ŵakutupulika ito ŵadada;
Ŵakupulika ito.
4 Dada withu wakharenge nase,
Kutovwira, kutumbika ta ŵanthu;
Kutumbika ta ŵanthu.
