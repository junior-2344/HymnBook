Dazi likunjira kukufipa
Herbert Stephen Irons (1834-1905)

1 Dazi likunjira kukufipa;
’We wa mtima wane,
pareke chisopo.
2 Umo Khristu m’nyifwa ya mphinjika
Wakarombera Wiske
kumpokerera.
3 Ndipo nkhupereka mtima wane
Kwa Uyo mizimu
yikughumiramo.
4 Kuti upumule nadi-nadi,
Kwambura khumbo linji,
kweni la Iyo.
5 Ndilo lichitike nyengo yose,
Kose-kose, mu moyo panji
mu nyifwa.
6 Nanga ndikharenge – ndine chara,
Kweni ndiyo m’kutemwa na
m’mphavu zake.
