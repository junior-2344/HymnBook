Kwafuma sono dazi
Chretien Urhan (1790-1845)

1 Kwafuma sono dazi
Kuchanya kwacha che!
Ndapukwa mhanya uwo
Muhanya uweme.
Wanguti bi usiku,
Sono nchimpalala
M’charu cha uchindami
Cha Imanuel.
2 Chisimi cha kutemwa
Ndi Khristu Mwenecho;
Ndamwapo pano pasi
Ndamkhuta kuchanya
Lusungu lukuyana
Na Nyanja yisani,
M’charu cha uchindami
Cha Imanuel.
3 Yesu wakunditemwa,
Naneso nkhumtemwa;
Nda mheni wandinjizga
M’nyumba ya monesko;
Ndiyo nkhumugomezga,
Yunji ntha nkhum’manya,
Nesi mu charu icho
Cha Imanuel.
4 Wandikorera wezi
Mu nthowa yose ’yi;
Masozi ghane nagho
Wazgora Chimwemwe;
Ndarumba Khristu pano:
Ndimrumbeso mura,
M’charu cha uchindami
Cha Imanuel.
5 Ndasangamo virwani
Minga na muhanya;
Sono ndajumpha vyose,
Fumu yandikhozga:
Ndindimbenge marumbo
Pamo na vilimbi,
M’charu cha uchindami
Cha Imanuel.
