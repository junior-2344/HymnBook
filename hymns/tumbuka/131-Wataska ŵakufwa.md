Wataska ŵakufwa
W.H. Doane

1 Wataska ŵakufwa,
ŵapwererera;
Uŵapoke ku uheni wawo
Lirira ŵasovyi, wuska ŵakuwa,
Ŵaphalira vya Yesu Mponoski.
Ŵataska wakufwa, ŵapwererera,
Yesu ngwa lusungu kuponoska.
2 Nanga ŵakumtinkha
wakuŵalinda,
Kupokerera awo ŵapera;
Uŵaŵeyerere na pamphu lose.
Wakupera tiŵagowokere.
3 Awo mbakupyoka
na m’rwani wawo
Ntha ŵakumanya kuwereramo;
Wezi na kutemwa vingawachizga,
Ŵangendaso na nkhongono zipya.
4 Wataska ŵakufwa ndi mlimo wako!
Yesu tiwakupe mphavu zake;
Ku nthowa yifinyi ŵawezgeraso.
Yesu wakafwira ŵa mayinga.
