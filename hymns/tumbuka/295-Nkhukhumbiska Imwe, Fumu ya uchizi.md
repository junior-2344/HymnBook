Nkhukhumbiska Imwe, Fumu ya uchizi
Robert Lowry (1826-99)

1 Nkhukhumbiska Imwe, Fumu ya uchizi;
Imwe mwekha pera mukupa chimango.
Nkhukhumba Imwe,
sono na mazuŵa ghose;
Mundovwire A Yesu,
nkhwiza kwa Imwe.
2 Nkhukhumbiska Imwe,
mukhare na ine;
Ntha ndingatondeka para muli nane.
3 Nkhukhumbiska Imwe,
m’kukondwa, m’kuwinya;
Ndiwurenge Imwe,
Uzima ngwa waka.
4 Nkhukhumbiska Imwe,
A Yesu,mwa’tuwa;
Mundikumbukenge,
ndiwe mwana winu.
