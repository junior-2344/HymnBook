Charu chose ncha Chiuta na ivyo virimo
Thomas Clark (1775-1859) | Salmo 24

1 Charu chose ncha Chiuta na ivyo virimo;
Mafuko ghose gha ŵanthu nvilengiwa vyake.
2 Ndiyo wakachikhazika na kuchikhozga swi;
Vyaru vvavizingirizga na mbwani yikuru.
3 Ndi njani uyo wangasenderera kwa Iyo?
Ndi munthu nju wangakhara mu tempele lake?
4 Ndi mwene uyo waphotora mawoko ghakhe,
Ŵambura kujikuzga na wambura uryarya.
5 Wakunyoroka Chiuta wamkuŵatumbika
Ŵa magomezgeko ŵake wamkuŵapwerera.
