Yesu tiri kutali
Arranged by Arthur Seymour Sullivan (1842-1900)

1 Yesu tiri kutali
na kungweruka kwinu;
Tatayika m’uheni;
m’tipulike Fumu.
2 Tilirire uheni, mu mphavu
za kuchanya
Titondenge mu nkhondo;
m’tipulike Fumu.
3 M’tipe kujiyuyura kuti
tiŵambalenge
Zinkhoka za ŵarwani;
m’tipulike Fumu.
4 M’tingweruskire chisi;
m’rongore khumbo linu;
M’savye uheni withu;
m’tipulike Fumu.
5 M’tipe vinjeru vyinu
kusungirira ise
Mu mtende na kusangwa;
m’tipulike Fumu.
6 Wezi winu utonde mtafu
wa ise tose;
M’tirongozge kuchanya;
m’tipulike Fumu.
