Mponoski, nga ndi muliska
W.B. Bradbury

1 Mponoski, nga ndi muliska,
m’tirongozge makora;
M’luhari lwinu luweme;
tiŵe m’lukutu lwinu.
Fumu Yesu, Fumu Yesu
tiri ŵinu, mwagula.
2 Mutisungirire m’nthowa,
muŵe mubwezi withu;
M’tiponoske ku uheni,
m’tiwezge ku kuzgeŵa,
Fumu Yesu, Fumu Yesu,
m’tipulike m’kuromba.
3 Mwalayizga kutitora,
nanga tiŵe ŵaheni;
Mu na wezi kuponoska,
na mphavu kuphotora,
Fumu Yesu, Fumu Yesu,
ta ŵana tiwerenge.
4 Tipenje thumbiko linu;
tichite khumbo linu;
Ndimwe mwekha Mtaski withu,
m’zure m’mitima yithu,
Fumu Yesu, Fumu Yesu,
mwatitemwa, m’temwenge.
