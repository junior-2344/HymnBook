Dazi la Mzimu, Mtaski ‘mwe!
Katholiches Gesangbuch, Vienna, c. 1774

1 Dazi la Mzimu, Mtaski ‘mwe!
Mulipo, mdima palive;
Bingu la pasi lireke
Kumujazgani kwa ine.
2 Na mise, apa nkhugona,
Ndipukwe nadi ku mtima
Kugona tulo twa Mzimu
Pa chipakato cha Yesu.
3 Kharani nane na mhanya;
Kwambura Imwe ndingafwa.
Kharani nane na mise,
Ndireke kuŵa mu chisi.
4 Usange yumo wasovya,
Wapata lero wanagwa,
Mumwezge, wapere sono,
Wangagonanga m’kwananga.
5 Ŵarwari muŵalindenge,
Ŵakavu muŵasambazge,
Tulo twa ŵaranda tuŵe
Tuweme nga twa ŵam’jedu.
6 M’titumbike pa kuwuka,
M’tisungirire pa kwenda:
Tisuke tibire tose
M’chindimba cha wezi winu.
