Fumu yikutumbike
Henry John Gauntlett (1805-76) | Fumu yikutumbike, | Fumu yiŵariske chisko pa iwe, | yikupenge uchizi; | Fumu yiwusizge maso pa iwe, | yikupe mutende. Amen.

1. MWAMVA Mawu a Mulungu; 
Mukabvomeranjiko? 
Kodi musandula mtima 
Ndi kumvera Yesuyo? 
Bvomerani, Bvomerani 
Bvomerani msangatu, 
Musazengereze ayi, 
Landirani Yesuyo. 
2. Munachita nthantha kale, 
Musazengereze ’yi; 
Yesu akulindirani, 
Sadzakana inu ’yi. 
3. Musaope pokumbuka 
Zakuipa anuzo; 
Khulupilirani Yesu, 
Adzachotsa zonsezo 
4. Perekani mtima wanu 
Kwa Mulungu wathuyo; 
Khulupirirani Yesu
