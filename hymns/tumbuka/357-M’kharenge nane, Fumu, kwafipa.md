M’kharenge nane, Fumu, kwafipa
William Henry Monk (1823-89)

1 M’kharenge nane, Fumu, kwafipa;
Chisi nchikuru, muŵenge nane;
Para wovwiri ŵanji wazgewa
Ndimwe Mvwiri wane, muŵe nane.
2 Umoyo wa m’charu ntha ngwa ndanda,
Viweme na vimwemwe vimare;
Vikuzgoka na kuvunda vyose;
’Mwe ntha mukuzgoka, muŵe nane.
3 Nkhumukhumbani mazuwa ghose,
Wezi winu ukutonda mrwani;
Chirongozgi, nchirikizgi ndimwe,
Mu chisi na mhanya muŵe nane.
4 Ntha nkhopaso para muli nane,
Vyakuwinya ntha nkhuvipulika;
Nyifwa na thegha ntha vikofyaso,
Nkhuviruska uvwe muŵe nane.
5 Pa kufwa murongore mphinjika,
Muŵare m’chisi mundirongozge;
Kuchanya kuche, pasi pafipe,
Mu wumi na m’nyifwa, muŵe nane.
