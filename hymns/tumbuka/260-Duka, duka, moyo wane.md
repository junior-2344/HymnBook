Duka, duka, moyo wane
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Duka, duka, moyo wane,
sangurukira Mponoski,
Kuwombola kwa Yehova
kwabulika m’charu chako.
Chiuta watunthumura
chiponosko m’charu chako
Wakwimikira kuwombokwa.
Wakubenekera me Muzimu
wane wafunda
Lulutiranga moyo wane.
2 Vina, vina, moyo wane
lulutira mtima wane,
Mulenji wa ku Yehova
watumphuka m’charu chako.
3 Heneranga moyo wane,
marazi ghakuwoneka,
Na chisi chikutemuka,
lulutira moyo wane.
4 Udemwere moyo wane,
ndi Yehova wakupukwa,
Wakwimikira lusengwe lwa
chiponosko, henera.
