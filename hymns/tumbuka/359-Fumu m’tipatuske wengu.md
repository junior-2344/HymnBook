Fumu m’tipatuske wengu
William Letton Viner (1790-1867)

1 Fumu m’tipatuske wengu;
m’tizuzge na kukondwa;
Tiŵe na kutemwa kwinu,
titondere mu wezi;
M’tikwimiske, m’tikwimiske,
ta ŵakwenda mu charu.
2 Tikuwonga na thumbiko
ivangeli la ndanda;
Vipambi vya chiponosko
vyandane mu mitima;
M’khare nase, m’khare nase,
lero na myirayira.
