M’charu cha kuchanya
Henry John Gauntlett (1805-76)

1 M’charu cha kuchanya
mulive usiku;
Mulimo ntha ungavuska;
mulimo nkhutemwa.
2 Mulive chitima,
zuŵa ndiweme ’mwe;
Masozi ghali m’vya kale
ivyo vyajumphapo.
3 Mulive uheni
m’wakutumbikika;
M’njirira wawo ngutuŵa;
lusumu nduweme.
4 Muliveso nyifwa;
awo wafikamo
Nyifwa ŵali kuyitonda,
ntha ŵatifwengeso.
5 Yesu m’tirongozge,
visuke vimare
Usiku, nyifwa, uheni –
kuchanya nkhwa ise.
