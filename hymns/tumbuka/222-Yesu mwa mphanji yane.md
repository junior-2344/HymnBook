Yesu mwa mphanji yane
Richard Redhead (1820-1901)

1 Yesu mwa mphanji yane,
ndibisame mwa Imwe;
Ndopa ’zo zikafuma
umo ŵakamrasani
Zindichizge ku mtima,
zinditaske m’kwananga.
2 Ntchito zane nzichoko,
ndikuru dango linu;
Nanga phamphu liwuke
kulirira kwananga,
Vyose vikuperera:
Mponoski ndimwe mwekha.
3 Ndilive na kaweme,
nkhukorako mphinjika;
M’ndichitire lusungu;
nda wa nkhuli m’vwarike;
Nda mukazuzi ine;
mundisavye, ndingafwa.
4 Ndichali na umoyo,
na m’kuzirika kwane,
Na pa kwiza kuchanya,
kumwonani m’chizumbe,
Yesu, mwa mphanji yane,
ndibisame mwa Imwe.
