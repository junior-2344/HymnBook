Ku mphinjika ya Yesu ndiko nkhudemerera
Freylinghausen's Gesangbuch, 1704

1 Ku mphinjika ya Yesu ndiko nkhudemerera,
Nanga nchakutemphentha chigomezgo chane.
2 Wakundikorerera nanga ine nkhuvuka,
Nkhusangwa pa kumanya wakundikorera.
3 Nkhujikama m’malundi gha Yesu na kuromba;
Kweni ndasangwa kuti wakundirombera.
4 Wakurombera ine, ’we ndiperere viŵi;
Nchindi yane ndi iyi, wakundirombera.
5 Ka ndingati nkhumtemwa? Kutemwa nkhuzizimu;
Mazgu ghakundiwuzga wakunditemweska.
6 Wakutemweska ine, nanga ndizizime ’mwe!
Mazgu gha ngakuzirwa, wakunditemweska.
