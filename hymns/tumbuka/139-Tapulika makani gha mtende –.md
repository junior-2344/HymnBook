Tapulika makani gha mtende –
Josiah Booth (1852-1929)

1 Tapulika makani gha mtende –
“Yesu ndiyo Mponoski wa ŵanthu.”
Mukaphare ku wose
Mukalowoke Nyanja;
Ndilo dango la Yesu Mponoski.
2 Mukakwere mapiri, muyeghe
Makani ghakusoka ŵaheni,
Mwa virwa m’nyanja mwimbe,
Mwa vyaru m’wezge kwimba,
Ŵanthu wose ŵamanye Mponoski.
3 Mwa ŵankhondo mwimbire Mponoski;
Wakafwa, wakughuma kwa ndada;
Para mtima wafipa pakupenja lusungu
Na pa nyifwa, mwimbire Mponoski.
4 Mphepo zichemerezge chomene,
’Mwe muzipokerere na kwimba
“Chiponosko chikuru
Chizira wose m’charu”
Ndusumu tikwimba Mponoski.
