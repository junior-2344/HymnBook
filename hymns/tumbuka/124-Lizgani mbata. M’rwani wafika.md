Lizgani mbata. M’rwani wafika
Wm. F. Sherwin

1 Lizgani mbata. M’rwani wafika:
Mwimenge pamo na Fumu!
M’vware zikhore.
M’thaze na kwima.
Mtima m’wike pa mazgu ghake.
Wukani ‘mwe! Nkhondo za mphinjika,
Pizganinge mazgu gha Fumu.
Rutaninge na kwimba “Hosana”,
Khristu ndiyo Mrongozgi withu.
2 Tikumanenge na m’rwani withu;
Na kukhozgeka m’mtima;
M’tore mauta ghakutondera
Nakurwira uthaski winu.
3 A Chiuta ’mwe! M’tipulikenge,
M’uchizgi winu m’tovwire;
Na pa kumara kwa nkhondo iyi
Tikavware mphumphu ya moyo.
