Chiuta wane, Chiuta wane
E. Chavura

1 Chiuta wane, Chiuta wane,
mundichizge ine, (Kaŵiri)
M’zina la Yesu, Mwana winu,
Wakutemwa, wa lusungu,
mundichizge ine.
2 Chiuta wane, Chiuta wane,
mundigowokere,
M’zina la Yesu, Mwana winu,
Ndopa zake zindisuke
ku viheni vyane.
3 Chiuta wane, Chiuta wane,
mundipokerere,
M’zina la Yesu, Mwana winu,
Fumu yane ya lusungu,
mundipokerere.
4 Chiuta wane, Chiuta wane,
mundisungirire.
M’zina la Yesu, Mwana winu,
Mundithaske ku viheni;
mundisungirire.
