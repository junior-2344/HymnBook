Pulika, mtima wane!
John Bacchus Dykes (1823-76)

1 Pulika, mtima wane!
Ndi Muponoski wako;
Yesu wakufumba,
“Kasi ukunditemwa?”
2 “Nkhakuwombola iwe,
nkhapozga bamba lako,
Nkhukupenja mu chisi,
nkhakupa kungweruka
3 “Kasi nyina wa mwana
wangawura lusungu?
Inya wangaruwapo,
kweni nkhukukumbuka.
4 “Kutemwa kwane kose
nkhwambura kuzgokapo,
Nkhwakurutirira swi, nkhwa
wanangwa, nkhwa mphavu.
5 “Uti uwone Ine,
nga ukwizire wezi;
Tiukharenge nane; Kasi
ukunditemwa?”
6 Fumu, ndi na chitima:
nkhumtemwani pachoko.
Mundovwire na wezi,
ndimtemwani chomene.
