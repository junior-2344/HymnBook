Nadi nkhumanya Yesu ngwane!
Mrs. J.F. Knapp

1 Nadi nkhumanya Yesu ngwane!
Nkhuwona nadi uchindami!
Wandiwombola, nadi-nadi,
Ndopa zatozga mtima wane.
Iyi ndi sumu, sumu yane,
Iyo nkhuyimba nyengo yose;
Agha ndi mazgu, mazgu ghane
Yitumbikike Fumu yane!
2 Ndajipereka, mtima wose;
Sono wakhara mkati mwane;
Yesu walimo, nadi-nadi;
Wandirongora khumbo lake.
3 Ndina chimango m’mtima wane;
Yandikondweska Fumu yane;
Sono nkhusangwa, nadi-nadi;
Wandisungire nyengo yose.
