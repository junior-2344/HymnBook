Ndasuzgikanga zuwa lose m’charu mono
African Melody

1 Ndasuzgikanga zuwa lose m’charu mono,
Ndapulika mazgu ghakuti, “zanga kwane”.
Zanga kwane, zanga kwane mranda iwe;
Suzgo lose tilimalenge, zanga kwane.
2 Nanga ndendanga m’charu muno vyalemanga,
Fumu yane yikandichema, “zanga kwane”.
3 Katundu wane wandananga mpaka nyifwa,
Wamara ’mwe! Yesu wakati, “zanga kwane”.
4 Mutende uno ndawusanga mwaku Yesu,
Nchawanangwa Yesu wakati “zanga kwane”.
5 Mtembwere uwu ngwa pa m’tima na muzimu;
Kupumuzga nkhwa pamutima, “zanga kwane”.
