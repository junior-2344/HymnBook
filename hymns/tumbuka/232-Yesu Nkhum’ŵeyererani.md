Yesu Nkhum’ŵeyererani
Ira D. Sankey

1 Yesu nkhum’ŵeyererani,
Kwambura Imwe ndifwenge,
M’ndipe chiponosko chinu,
Mundipokerere.
Chorus
Mundipokerere umo ndiriri 'mwe;
Ndilive na chakwandula; 
mundipokerere.
2 Ndasokwa na zakwananga,
Kweni ndine mukafwira;
Nkhutondeka kujithaska;
M’ndithaske ndipera.
3 Nkhutondeka kujinozga,
Madazgo ghaweme nkhuswa
Ndipo uli m’ndithaskire,
Mu zina la Yesu.
4 Khuwa mu malundi ghinu,
Mundilengere lusungu,
Mlimo mwambe, muwufiske,
Mundipokerere.
