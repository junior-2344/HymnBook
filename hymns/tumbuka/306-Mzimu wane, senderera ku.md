Mzimu wane, senderera ku
Adapted from Ludwig Spohr 1784-1859

1 Mzimu wane, senderera ku
malo gha wezi,
Uwe m’malundi gha Yesu,
ntha ungafwa kuko.
2 Na pangano linu, Fumu,
ndenderera kwinu;
Muzimu wakuzotopa ndiwo
mukuchema.
3 Mphingo ya uheni wane
yandivunamiska;
Nkhondo yiri m’mtima wane,
chimango mundipe.
4 Muŵe mphanji yane, Fumu,
ndibisamengemo,
Kuti ndimanye kuchintha
ku mphachi yikali.
5 Kutemwa kwakutondweska,
kuyegha mphinjika!
Kuti ŵaheni nga ndine
ŵasange uchizi.
