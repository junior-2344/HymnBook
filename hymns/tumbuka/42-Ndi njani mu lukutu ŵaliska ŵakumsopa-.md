Ndi njani mu lukutu ŵaliska ŵakumsopa?
Benjamin Russel Hanby (1833-67)

1 Ndi njani mu lukutu ŵaliska ŵakumsopa?
Ndi Fumu; nchakuzukuma;
ndi Fumu Themba likuru;
Tiyitumbike tose;
tiyivwarike mphumphu.
2 Ndi njani wakuchita mlimo na chitatata?
3 Ndi njani ku thengere wakuyezgeka kuko?
4 Ndi njani ’kulilira Lazaro pa mararo?
5 Ndi njani, usiku bi, ’kuromba m'Getsemane?
6 Ndi njani pa Kalvari ’kurombera ŵarwani?
7 Ndi njani wakazguka kwiza katiponoska?
8 Ndi njani wakuwusa ŵa pasi na ŵa mchanya.
