Chiuta mwenecho wa mtambo na pasi
Rev. J.P. Chirwa

1 Chiuta mwenecho wa mtambo na pasi,
Mbwani na charu ndi nyumba yinu pera.
2 Ŵanthu ŵamuzengerani nyumba iyi;
Tikuyipereka kwinu, mupokere.
3 Mzimu winu wakumane nawo ŵose
Awo ŵakumusopani m’nyumba iyi.
4 M’nyumba iyi ŵakusoŵa ŵawoneke;
M’nyumba iyi ŵa mtafu ŵapulikire.
5 Awo ŵali kupuruka wawezgeke
Ŵasanguruskike awo mba chitima
6 Wakutepatepa ŵasange nkhongono
Ŵagomezge, ŵalchore, ŵate nganganga
