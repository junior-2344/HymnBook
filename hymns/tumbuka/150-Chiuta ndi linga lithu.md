Chiuta ndi linga lithu
M.Tembo

1 Chiuta ndi linga lithu
lakukhora nkhanira nkhanira,
Wali mchanya kutovwira
para ise tikuzingiziwa.
Ndiyo ise tikutamira;
ndiyo chipokwero chithu;
Tichimbirirenge kwa Iyo
para tikuzingiziwa.
2 Ntheura tileke kopa nanga
charu chingatizgokera,
Chifukwa Chiuta withu
ngwakutitondera maurwani.
3 Uyo wana suzgo m’thupi
wamucheme na chipulikano,
Uyo wamatirwa m’kati mu
kwananga wamucheme sono.
4 Aleluya tina Nkhoswe
yambura daka nesi khonthoro,
Mwimbirire, M’lulutire
imwe muli m’linga la kukhora.
