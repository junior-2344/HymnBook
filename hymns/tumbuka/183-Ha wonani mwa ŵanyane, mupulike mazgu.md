Ha wonani mwa ŵanyane, mupulike mazgu
Adapted by Robert Simpson (1790-1832) | Salmo 78

1 Ha wonani mwa ŵanyane, mupulike mazgu,
Agho nkhuyowoya namwe, ndigho nga umoyo.
2 Nkhuyowoyera Chiuta, umo wakizira,
kwiza kakhara na ŵanthu kuŵa Mponoski.
3 Ndigho ise tapulika; ŵasekulu ŵithu
Ŵakatisambizga kale; namwe mupulike.
4 Ŵalero nawo ŵamanye lusungu lwa Dada,
Na umo wakatifwira Yesu Mtaski withu.
5 Makani gharutirire, ghakaŵafikire
Awo ŵakwiza munyuma, nawo ŵize kwake.
