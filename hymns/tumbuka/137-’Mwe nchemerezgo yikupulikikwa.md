’Mwe nchemerezgo yikupulikikwa
Chas. H. Gabriel (arr.)

1 ’Mwe nchemerezgo yikupulikikwa,
“Mutume muliko!”
Kuli ŵanthu wakwenera kupona;
“Mutume muliko!”
Mwe mutumeko Ivangeli; lipharike ku wose;
'Mwe mtumeko Ivangeli; liŵarenge kwa ndanda.
2 Tapulika nchemerezgo ya ŵanthu
“Mutume muliko!”
Tikupereka sembe pa mphinjika;
“Mutume muliko!”
3 Uchizgi wa Yesu utandazgike;
“Mutume muliko!”
Ukhristu wandanenge m’charu chose;
“Mutume muliko!”
4 Tireke kuvuka kumulimira;
“Mutume muliko!”
Apo tikuwunganya vyuma vyake;
“Mutume muliko!”
