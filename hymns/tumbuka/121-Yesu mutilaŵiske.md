Yesu mutilaŵiske
Johann Rodolph Ahle (1625-73). Arranged by Johann Sebastian | Bach (1685-1750)

1 Yesu mutilaŵiske
ta ŵakuwungana muno;
Mwize mutisambizge,
tikumulindirirani;
Vya charu tivipatenge,
tipulike Imwe pera.
2 Tose tikukhumbiska
kungweruskika na Imwe;
Para mungatinora mu
mudima tingaranda;
Muwusenge mazgu ghithu,
na ntchito, na makhumbiro.
3 Dazi la Chiuta ’mwe!
Mwe utozi wa ŵadada!
Mutirongosolere, tiŵe
mchanya kusambira:
Mu kusopa uku kose mitima
yingweruskike.
