Lizgu la Yehova withu ndiweme chomene
Thomas Tallis (1510-85) | Wonaniso Sumu 7

1 Lizgu la Yehova withu ndiweme chomene;
Likuchimbizga mudima nda nkhongono nadi.
2 Ilo likutisambizga pa kumanya Yesu;
Makani ghakurukuru likutimanyiska.
3 Ilo likutingweruska, ndilo dazi lithu;
Ilo likutirongozga m’nthowa yithu yose.
4 Tose tili pulikizge, tilitegherezge;
Tilikore mu mitima, tilitemwe ilo.
