Wanena ndi Yesu mazgu ghose agha
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Wanena ndi Yesu mazgu ghose agha;
Fumu ya chimango yarapa nakuti:
Nkhumfumya chara, nkhumtaya chara ‘yo wiza,
Nkhumfumya chara, nkhumtaya chara uyo wiza kwane.

3 Zaninge kwa Yesu imwe mwapuluka;

Mwasuzgikirachi? Mponoski ndi Yesu.
