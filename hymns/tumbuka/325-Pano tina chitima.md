Pano tina chitima
T. Bilby

1 Pano tina chitima,
pano tina kuŵinya
Kuchanya kulive.
Chamkuŵa Chimwemwe,
Chimwemwe, Chimwemwe,
Chamkuŵa Chimwemwe.
Ntha tam’kupatukana.
2 Ŵakumutemwa pano, uvwe
ŵafwa, ŵamkwimba
Kuchanya kwa Yesu.
3 Twana twamkuŵa nayo,
uto tukumuromba
Mu sukulu zose.
4 Ŵaneni ŵithu wose,
na ŵasambizgi wuwo;
Ntha ŵampatukana.
5 Kura tamkuchaso ‘mwe’
na Mtaski pa chizumbe,
Wakuchindikika.
6 Uko tamkwimba tose,
tikamwonge kwa ndanda
Yesu Fumu yithu.
