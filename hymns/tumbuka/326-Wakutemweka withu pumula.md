Wakutemweka withu pumula
Ira D. Sankey

1 Wakutemweka withu pumula;
Goneka mutu wako pa Yesu,
Uyo wakukutemweska nadi:
Gona.
2 Ukugona chete nga ndutema;
Ntha tiuwuke kuzukikaso;
Ukupumula m’chimango chene;
Gona.
3 Yisuke yimarenge mifwiri,
Wasuke waronge vyake m’nthamba,
Usuke urute m’nkholomera,
Gona.
4 Usuke wize mlenje wa Fumu,
Ŵasuke ŵazgukenge ŵanyithu,
Pakwiza kwake mu uchindami,
Gona.
5 Tisuke tivware vya ku Yesu,
Nakukumanaso pa chizumbe,
Timanye umo tamanyikwira,
Gona.
