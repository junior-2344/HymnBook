Themba la uchindami pa mtima uheni
St. Gall Gesangbuch, 1863.

1 Themba la uchindami pa mtima uheni
Likwima na kulanga mayigha mukati;
Mabumbwe gha urwirwi ghawerera nyuma;
Pa kupulika Themba kuti, “Kuŵe bata!”
2 Panyake likunena: Ehe! Vyachitika;
Mu nkhondo wakuvivya kwambura mahomwa;
Taŵanthu pakuwona, kuzizwa na kucha:
Mitima yinonono yalamba kwa Yesu.
3 Kuvwara che likwiza, na mphumphu pa chene;
Vijaro vya mikuŵa, mimphinga ya chuma,
Vyam’jurikira vyekha; ŵamkoli ŵafuma,
Lwandi wose ŵakwimba, “Khristu ndiyo Fumu.”
4 Panyakeso likwiza wengu-wengu pera;
Na kupwererera mazgu mu khutu la mheni;
Wakulinda na mpheska kuzgora kwa munthu;
Wakufumbaso ruta, “Tiufwirengechi?”
5 Mukumancha kutemwa, uchizi ngukuru,
’Mwe Khristu! Chisko chinu chikugadima tu!
Mtima withu ukucha kuwona kutemwa;
Tikwiza na zisumu kukhara na Imwe.
