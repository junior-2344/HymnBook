Ŵadada, ŵadada, tiri pano
African Melody

1 Ŵadada, ŵadada, tiri pano
Tizamkumurombani Imwe.
2 M’pulike,m’pulike,Fumu yithu
Ivyo titirombe kwa Imwe.
3 Taŵeya, taŵeya, mutumbike
Uwu mpingo winu uweme.
4 Tap’lika, tapulika kulira
Kwa ŵanthu ŵambura kumanya.
5 Ŵakwenda, ŵakwenda mu zinthowa
Zakurata ku pharaniko.
6 Ŵanandi, ŵanandi ŵakumara,
Ŵambura kumanya na Yesu.
7 Ŵawuke, ŵawuke ŵanthu ŵinu,
Kuti ŵaŵe mathenga ghinu.
8 Mutume,mutume wasambizgi,
Ŵapharenge mazgu ghaweme.
9 Wizenge, wizenge maluŵiro
Ufumu winu m’charu chose.
