A Chiuta mukalenga
Samuel Webber (1740-1816)

1 A Chiuta mukalenga
ŵanthu aŵo ŵakudanga,
Msweni yumo, mwori
yumo, aŵo ŵose n’thupi limo.
2 Ŵabali aŵa ŵanyithu
ŵiza panthazi pa Imwe,
Ŵazomerezgana kuti
ŵakwatiske mu ukhristu.
3 Muŵovwire, Mlengi withu,
ŵakhozge pangano laŵo:
Ŵatemwane, ŵovwirani,
ntha ŵangapatukanaso.
4 Wagomezge Fumu yithu
yiŵenge movwiri waŵo,
Yitumbike nyumba yaŵo
na mu kwenda kwawo kose.
