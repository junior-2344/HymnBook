Zaninge, ŵakusuzgika
J.H. Stockton

1 Zaninge, ŵakusuzgika;
na uheni winu;
Fumu yithu nja lusungu,
tiyimpumuzgani.
Gomezgani, gomezgani,
mwa Yesu Khristu;
Wati waponoske mose;
wakumtemwani.
2 Wakathira ndopa zake
pa kufwira ŵanthu;
Ndopa izo zikathika
zikum’toweskani.
3 Yesu nadi ndi nenesko na
chimango chinu;
Pulikanani na Iyo,
musangurukenge.
4 Njirani mose mu mpingo
wake wakutowa;
Tirute tose kuchanya
kukhara na Yesu.
