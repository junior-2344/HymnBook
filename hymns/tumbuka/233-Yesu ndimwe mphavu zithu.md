Yesu ndimwe mphavu zithu
P. Banda

1 Yesu ndimwe mphavu zithu,
Ise tiri ŵa mu charu;
M’ze pafupi, m’titumbike.
2 Fumu, ise tikuzizwa
Na ivyo mukuyowoya;
Zani Imwe m’tisambizge.
3 Ise tikuwonga chara
Zina linu ili Yesu,
Zani Imwe m’tikumbuske.
4 Ŵanthu ŵatayika wose
Nga mberere zakusoŵa,
Izo zilivye muliska.
5 Yesu mchezgi wa ŵaheni,
M’sungirire mpingo winu
Uwo uli m’charu chose.
