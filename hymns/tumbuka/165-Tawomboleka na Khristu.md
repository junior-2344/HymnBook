Tawomboleka na Khristu
Wittenberg Gesangbuch, 1524

1 Tawomboleka na Khristu,
tawezgekera mwa Iyo,
Tikuyithuwuska nyifwa;
wasuke wize, wizeso.
2 Thupi lakutimenyekera
lawoneka mu chingwa,
Likukuzga chitemwano,
wasuke wize, wizeso.
3 Ndopa zake tikuwona,
zikathikira ta ŵanthu,
Mpheska zikuzirongora,
wasuke wize, wizeso.
4 Usiku wura wa mnkhorwe
na dazi la kucha kwake
Vikukumana m’monesko,
wasuke wize, wizeso.
5 Chigomezgo cha mwaŵi mwe!
Tirekenge kuwa nkhumbi;
Tikhome m’tima, tikhazge,
wasuke wize, wizeso.
