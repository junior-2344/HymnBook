Nako nkhunozga, A Yesu, kukumbuka Imwe
Jacob Bacchus Dykes, 1823-76

1 Nako nkhunozga, A Yesu, kukumbuka Imwe,
Kweni kumwonani chisko ndiko nkhwakuzirwa.
2 Palive mazgu ghangimba, palive mutima
Ungasanga zina linji lakuruska linu.
3 Gomezgo la ŵakupera, kondwa la'kuzika,
Mukawafwira lusungu ŵakumupenjani.
4 Ku ŵakumsangani nchichi? Ntha tingarongora,
Umo ŵakuŵatemwera, wake ŵakumanya
5 Yesu nchimwemwe chithu ’mwe, muŵe njombe yithu;
Nchindi yithu ndimwe sono, na muyirayira.
