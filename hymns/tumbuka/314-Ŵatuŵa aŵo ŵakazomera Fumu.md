Ŵatuŵa aŵo ŵakazomera Fumu
Ralph Vaughan Williams (1872-1958)

1 Ŵatuŵa aŵo ŵakazomera Fumu
yawo panthazi pa ŵanthu.
Sono kuchanya wakupumula.
Aleluya!
2 Mukaŵa linga laŵo la mphavu,
Chirongozi mu nkhondo yiweme.
Mu mudima mukaŵangweruska,
Aleluya!
3 Wankhondo ŵinu ŵime mitima,
ŵarwenge nga mbatuŵa ŵa kale
wapokere mphumphu ya kutonda,
Aleluya!
4 Ŵakale sono kura ŵakucha,
ta ŵa pasi tichali kulimba;
Kweni tose tiri ŵinu pamo,
Aleluya!
5 Para nkhondo njakofya chomene,
sumu yaŵo ya kutonda yize;
Tamkukondwera na kukhozgeka,
Aleluya!
6 Ku zambwe dazi likutezuka,
wankhondo ŵapumule luŵiro
Mba mwaŵi wakurata kuchanya,
Aleluya!
7 Tamkusekerera kucha kupya,
ŵatuŵa wamkuwunjikanaso;
Themba la uchindami lamkwiza,
Aleluya!
8 Ŵamkufumira ku mphaka zose,
wamkunjira mryango wa kuchanya,
Kwimbira Wiske, Mwana na Mzimu,
Aleluya!
