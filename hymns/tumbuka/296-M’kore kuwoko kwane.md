M’kore kuwoko kwane
Hubert P. Main

1 M’kore kuwoko kwane,
’mwe Mponoski!
Ndilije kumanya kwenda ndekha;
M’nditeteske, ndipo uheni wose
Ntha ukumanya kunditenthemya.
2 M’kore kuwoko kwane,
’mwe Mponoski!
Mundiguzire kwa Imwe mwekha;
Kwambura Imwe tindipurukenge,
Nkhopa kwenda ndizire ndingawa.
3 M’kore kuwoko kwane,
nthowa yane
Kwambura Imwe nja mdima pera;
Kweni mu chisko chinu chakuŵara
Ndina kungweruka na chimwemwe.
4 M’kore kuwoko kwane!
Para nkhwiza
Ku mronga uwo m’kandambukira,
Mujeduske maji ghafipa Imwe,
Na kugoneka mayigha ghose.
