Charu nchakucha ’cho, cha kuchanya
Selected Melodies, 1827

1 Charu nchakucha ’cho, cha kuchanya;
Ŵazgoka ŵatuŵa mbenecharu;
Ŵakwimba sumu ’yo,
’Mtaski withu ndi mkuru;
Thokozaninge ’mwe, m’ruta-ruta.
2 Mwize ku charu ’cho cha vimwemwe;
Mungakayikanga, mungadonda.
Tamsekerera ’ko,
Mauheni kulive,
Tamkhara na Yesu m’ruta-ruta.
3 Maso ku charu ’cho ghakuzota;
Mwenecho watemwa ŵana ŵake;
Tiyeni, tendenge
Ku ’chindami wa kura,
Uko nkhwakuzirwa m’ruta-ruta.
