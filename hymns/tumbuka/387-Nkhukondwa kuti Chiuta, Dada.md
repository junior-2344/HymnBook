Nkhukondwa kuti Chiuta, Dada
P.P. BIiss

1 Nkhukondwa kuti Chiuta, Dada,
wandinenenera wakunditemwa;
mazgu ghaweme, ghakuzizwiska,
ghakuti Yesu wakunditemwa.
Yesu wakunditemwa ine,
Inya ine, inya ine;
Nkhusekera, wakunditemwa,
Yesu wakutemwa.
2 Para nkhum’ruwa na kupuruka,
Wakundipenja, wakunditemwa;
Ndiwererenge ku Fumu yane,
Na kukumbuka kutemwa kwake.
3 Ndina chimango chikuru lero;
Pakugomezga nkhutumbikika;
Satana wakutenthema nadi
Pakuti Yesu wakunditemwa.
4 Uvwe mwafumba – nkhumanya uli?
Nkhurumba Yesu, nkhumanya nadi;
Mzimu Mutuŵa wapanikizga,
Kuti Yesu wakutemwa ine.
