Kungweruka kwa mutende
Lowell Mason 1792-1872

1 Kungweruka kwa mutende,
Kwangweruska ta ŵanthu;
Fukatirani ŵaranda mu zinyanja;
Fukatirani ŵaranda mu zinyanja.
2 Kungweruka kwangweruska
Awo ŵakaŵa m’chisi;
Fukatirani ŵaranda mu zinyanja;
Fukatirani ŵaranda mu zinyanja.
3 Mugomezge kungweruka
Mkati m’vipakalala;
Kwamkumovwirani imwe mu zinyanja;
Kwamkumovwirani imwe mu zinyanja.
4 Kungweruka kukutaska,
Kukovwira ŵaranda;
Tamkukumanizga vipyo mu zinyanja;
Tamkukumanizga vipyo mu zinyanja.
