Yesu wali kwiza; ujipulikire
M. Tembo

1 Yesu wali kwiza; ujipulikire;
Wachema ŵanthu wose; ujipulikire.
Haleluya! Ujipulikire.
2 Kumanya ukumanya; ujipulikire;
Kulive udapizi; ujipulikire.
3 Nkharamba zatumika; ujipulikire;
Zikupharazga Yesu; ujipulikire.
4 Yesu ndi Mponoski; ujipulikire;
Wa charu ichi chose; ujipulikire.
5 Kupulika wapulika; ujipulikire;
Ntha wamukupandira; ujipulikire.
6 Zaninge imwe mose; mujipulikire;
Mupokerere wumi; mujipulikire.
