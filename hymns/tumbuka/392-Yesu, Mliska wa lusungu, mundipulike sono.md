Yesu, Mliska wa lusungu, mundipulike sono
John Stainer (1840-1901) | Wonaniso sumu 391

1 Yesu, Mliska wa lusungu, mundipulike sono,
Ndine kamwana katema, zani kunditumbika.
2 Mukharenge nane sono apo nkhugona tulo,
Mundisungirire, m’chisi na m’maurwani ghose.
3 Mwandisungirira lero , ndipo nkhumuwongani,
Mwandivwarika, mwalera; m’tegherezge, nkhuromba.
4 Mundigowokere vyose; mutumbike ŵanyane,
Mundirongozge kuchanya, ndikharenge na Imwe.
