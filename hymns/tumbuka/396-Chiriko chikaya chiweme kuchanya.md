Chiriko chikaya chiweme kuchanya
James Sherman Tyler (1842-1917)

1 Chiriko chikaya chiweme kuchanya;
Kulije kanthu kakukazuzga
Kangaya kanjira.
2 Muponoski nkhwiza, nkumurombeskani,
Mundisukiske, mundisukiske,
Ku uheni wane.
3 Ndiŵe mwana winu kufuma ku lero;
M’ndisungirire, m’ndisungirire,
Ku kusovya kwane.
4 Ndisuke nkhimemo na ŵakuwombokwa,
Nda wakutowa, nda wakutowa,
M’charu cha mulere.
