Chiuta withu tikupulika, (kaŵiri)
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Chiuta withu tikupulika, (kaŵiri)
M’kuchemerezga mafuko ghose.
2 Mose m’pulike mazgu gha Fumu
Ghakuchemerezga m’kati mwinu.
3 Mose ryaninge mazgu gha fumu,
Nchakurya chinu, ŵanthu ŵa mpingo.
4 Yesu ndi mphanda ya mpingo wose
Mphanda ndi Yesu, mvwiri wa charu.
5 Wakataskanga ise ŵaheni,
Pa kwiza kwake muno mu charu.
