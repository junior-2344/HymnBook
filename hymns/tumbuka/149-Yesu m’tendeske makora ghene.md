Yesu m’tendeske makora ghene
Adam Drese (1620-1701)

1 Yesu m’tendeske makora ghene;
Nanga nthowa njakuvuska,
Timrondezgani, Fumu;
M’titeteskere kwithu kuchanya.
2 Nthowa njakofya, m’rwani wakhazga,
Kweni tirekenge kopa,
Tikhomenge m’chigomezgo,
Kuti tikuya kwithu kuchanya.
3 Para vitima vikutikoma,
Mu visuzgo na malwavyo,
Mukuzge chikosa, chithu,
Mutiwoneske kwithu kuchanya.
4 Yesu m’tendeske makora ghene;
Chirongozgi wa kuchanya
M’chiske, m’taske, mudangire,
Tisuke time m’charu cha Dada.
