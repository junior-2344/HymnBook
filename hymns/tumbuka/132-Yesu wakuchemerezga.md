Yesu wakuchemerezga
Jean Jacques Rousseau (1712-78)

1 Yesu wakuchemerezga,
“Ndi njani tiwarute
Kuya kanditeŵetera pa
kuvuna vyakurya?”
Fumu yikukoromoka,
yikupereka njombe,
Ndi njani watiyizgore?
“Ndine pano, ndituma.”
2 Para ulive kumanya
kuruta kutaliko.
Kuya kapenja ŵalemwa,
ungovwira ŵa m’fupi;
Nanga uŵe m’kavu, mupa
umo ukumanyira;
nvyakuzirwa kwa Iyo.
3 Uvwe watondeka kuŵa
musambizgi wa ŵanthu
Urongore nkharo yipya
yakurata kuchanya;
Mukuromba na wanangwa
ufiskirenge dango
Kumovwira munthu yose
umo ukumwonera.
4 Urekenge kuyowoya kuti,
“ Ndilive mlimo,”
Para ŵanandi ŵakufwa,
na Fumu yikuchema;
Kora m’limo na kukondwa
uwo yiri kukupa,
Zgora maluŵiro, uti,
“Ndine pano, ndituma.”
