Kale a Chiuta, mukatemwa charu,
African Melody

1 Kale a Chiuta, mukatemwa charu,
Mukatuma Yesu, Muponoski withu;
Titumbike Yesu, titumbike Yesu,
Titumbike Yesu.
2 Lero pano pasi wiza Muponoski,
Kukhara na ŵanthu, kuti ŵaŵachizge,
Mu uheni wawo, mu uheni wawo,
Mu uheni wawo.
3 Ndopa za Mwomboli zikamara m’randu
Uwo tikananga kale kwa Chiuta;
Gomezgani Yesu, gomezgani Yesu,
Gomezgani Yesu.
4 Lero Yesu wana lusungu lukuru;
Zuwa lenelino wakupenja wose,
Kuti ŵaŵe ŵakhe, kuti ŵaŵe wakhe.
Kuti ŵaŵe ŵakhe.
5 Zuŵa linelira tayam’kwima tose
Panthazi pa Yesu, tikapataule
Ivyo tachitanga, Ivyo tachitanga,
Ivyo tachitanga.
