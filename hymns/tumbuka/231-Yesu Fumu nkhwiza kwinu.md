Yesu Fumu nkhwiza kwinu
African Melody

1 Yesu Fumu nkhwiza kwinu,
Munditumbikenge;
Ndajikama pasi pinu,
Nkhumulirirani.
Nkhumuririrani Fumu,
nkhumuririrani,
Ndajikama pasi pinu,
nkhumuririrani.
2 Mupereke Mzimu winu,
Wose ŵazguzgike;
Mu mitima yaŵo yose
Ŵang’anamukeso.
3 Mwa ŵanthu laŵiskaninge
Kura ku mphinjika;
Mupokere chiponosko
Para mwagomezga.
