Mponoski wane warongozgeka
Rev. P.Z Thole

1 Mponoski wane warongozgeka
Nga ndi mberere, m’tafu wane.
2 Wakarombanga mu Getsemane
Usiku wose, mtafu wane.
3 Mponoski wane wapayikika,
Wakita ndopa, mtafu wane.
4 Wakafwa papo pachamphinjika,
Wakasungika, mtafu wane.
5 Wakawukaso umo mu dindi,
Watonda nyifwa, mtafu wane.
6 Wali kuruta kuya kuchanya
Kundiŵeyera, mtafu wane.
7 Mponoski njumo mu charo chose
Wanditaska ku mtafu wane.
