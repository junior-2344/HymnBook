Ine nkhumchindika ruta
Isaac Smith (1740-1800) | Salmo 341 Wonaniso O.S. Psalter 9

1 Ine nkhumchindika ruta
Yehova Chiuta;
Mzimu wakujitumbira
mwa mponoski wane.
2 Zaninge timutamike
tose timwimbire;
Nkhampenjanga wandisanga,
ndiyo wandithaska.
3 Mba mwaŵi ŵakugomezga,
ntha wakuyuyuka;
Ungweru waŵaŵalira,
ntha ŵali na soni
4 Nthumi za Chiuta withu
zaŵazingirizga
Aŵo ŵakumsopa ruta,
ndiwo mba mutende.
5 Muyezge Fumu mumanye
ndiyo nja lusungu;
Yikuwapwerera wose
ŵakuyigomezga.
6 Makutu gha Mtatakuya
ghakutipulika;
Para tikumulirira;
zaninge timsope.
