Mungarazganga nkhonyoro
Prys's Psalms 1621. Rev. P.Z. Thole.

1 Mungarazganga nkhonyoro
ku ŵanthu ŵa soni,
Taŵa mtima wakupera
tikumuŵeyani.
2 Vipata vyinu nvya, mwazi
ku ŵakujinyumwa,
Mungatijariranga ‘mwe’
rekani tinjire.
3 Imwe Fumu mukumanya
machitiro ghithu,
Vyakubisika mu mtima
mukuwona vyose.
4 Ŵadada tikumpenjani,
tiza na masozi,
Nga mbana ŵakupuruka
ŵizira wiskewo.
5 Wezi, Fumu, wezi pera,
ndiwo tikukhumba,
Ndimwe Dada wa lusungu,
mutisanguruske.
