Umo mphala pakomirwa
Adapted from Ludwig Spohr (1784-1859) | Wonaniso Sumu 183

1 Umo mphala pakomirwa
yikupukwira maji,
Ndimoso ku Fumu yane ine
ndapukwira.
2 Mzimu wane ukudoka
Chiuta wa moyo;
Ndize kawoneka kwinu
pa uli, Chiuta?
3 Masozi ndazgora kurya,
usiku na mhanya;
Ŵanji ŵakuti, “Chiuta wako
wali kochi?”
4 Pa kukumbukira ivi
mtima ukumara,
Kuti ine nkharutanga
na mupingo kale.
5 Nkharongozgananga nawo
M’nyumba ya Chiuta,
Na Chimwemwe, na marumbo –
mpingo wakukondwa.
6 Lusoko lwathikirachi,
iwe moyo wane?
Gomezga Chiuta wako,
tindirumbenge swi.
