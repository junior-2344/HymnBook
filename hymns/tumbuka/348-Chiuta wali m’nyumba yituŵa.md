Chiuta wali m’nyumba yituŵa
Habakkuk 2:20 | Chiuta wali m’nyumba yituŵa, | Chiuta wali m’nyumba yituŵa, | Mwa ŵanthu mose mum’chindike; | Mwa ŵanthu mose mum’chindike; | Mose mum’chindike. Amen.

1. MWAMVA Mawu a Mulungu; 
Mukabvomeranjiko? 
Kodi musandula mtima 
Ndi kumvera Yesuyo? 
Bvomerani, Bvomerani 
Bvomerani msangatu, 
Musazengereze ayi, 
Landirani Yesuyo. 
2. Munachita nthantha kale, 
Musazengereze ’yi; 
Yesu akulindirani, 
Sadzakana inu ’yi. 
3. Musaope pokumbuka 
Zakuipa anuzo; 
Khulupilirani Yesu, 
Adzachotsa zonsezo 
4. Perekani mtima wanu 
Kwa Mulungu wathuyo; 
Khulupirirani Yesu
