Mukati mu charu ichi
African Melody

1 Mukati mu charu ichi
m’ndirongozge ndimwe Yesu,
Mukhare mu mtima wane,
yiŵe nyumbayinu kwa ndanda.
Fumu m’kore woko lane m’ndirongozge
M’ndichintiske, mundiratiske ku uchindami.
2 Nkhongono yinu yikuru iyo
m’kalengera vyose
Yindilenge mupya lero,
ndiŵe Beteli linu lero.
3 Mu utechitechi wane
nkhuskeperera kwa Imwe,
Kuti Imwe mundizgore
ndiŵe wa nkhongono mu Mzimu.
4 Ndekha ndiri mu urwani,
mundiŵighire luŵigho;
Muŵe m’nthazi; muŵe m’nyuma,
m’ndizingirizge nuku-nuku.
