Pereka mtima m’randa iwe
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Pereka mtima m’randa iwe,
ndi Yesu wakuwuchema,
Pereka mtima mkavu iwe,
ndi Yesu wakakufwira
Perekanga, perekanga
mtima’wo kwa ku Yesu,
Sono wali pa muryango
pereka mtima wako.
2 Juranga mtima muzga iwe,
Yesu wali pa mryango,
Juranga mtima muheni iwe,
Yesu wayegha viweme.
3 Thulanga mtima m’kana dango
ndi Yesu wachemerezga,
Thulanga mtima, tula mtima
ndi Yesu wakuwuchema.
4 Pereka mzimu wakwananga
kwa Yesu Mponoski wako,
Pereka mzimu wakusoŵa ndi
Yesu wawuchizgenge.
