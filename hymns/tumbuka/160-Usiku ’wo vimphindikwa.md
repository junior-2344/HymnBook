Usiku ’wo vimphindikwa
Adapted by Edward Miller (1731-1807)

1 Usiku ’wo vimphindikwa
vikamucherukiranga,
Pakurya na wasambiri,
Mtaski wakatora chingwa
2 Ndipo wakati wawonga
Mwenecho wa charu chose,
Wakamenya chingwa icho,
wakaŵapa ŵasambiri.
3 Wakati, “Ndi thupi lane
lakumenyekera ŵanthu;
Muryenge; mupe kuchita
kukondeska kutemwa kwane.”
4 Nkhombo nayo wakawuska,
nakuwerezga kuwonga;
Wakapereka kwa iwo, wakati,
“Mumwerengemo.
5 “Ndopa zane nkhuthirira
kuphotora ŵakwananga:
Zikukhozga chipangano,
nakuvumbura uchizgi.
6 “Mwazara kutemwa m’nkhombo,
wose ŵamwenge zituŵa:
Zithike ruta-ruta swi,
nchikumbusko cha nyifwa yane.”
