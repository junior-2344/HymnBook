Chiuta mukumanya kavviro
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Chiuta mukumanya kavviro
kane kose,
Pulikani, nkhumuweyani.
2 Para nkhaŵa mwanichi
ndimwe mukandilera.
3 Mu tulo, na muhanya,
mukundisungilira.
4 Ndasambira na Yesu,
nkhumtemwa, nkhumuromba.
5 Nkhukhumba kunjiramo
mu mbumba yinu, Dada.
6 Chiuta mundovwire
ndiŵenge mwana winu.
