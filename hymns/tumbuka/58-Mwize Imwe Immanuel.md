Mwize Imwe Immanuel
The Hymnal Noted, 1854. | Adapted by Thomas Helmore (1811-90)

1 Mwize Imwe Immanuel,
Mwize muthaske Israel,
Uyo ndi myeni pano pasi,
Wakulinda Mwana wa Chiuta.
Sekerera, Immanuel
Wakukwizira Israel.
2 Mwize ’mwe Themba likuru,
Ŵanthu ŵinu thaskaninge,
Ku nyifwa muŵaponoskenge,
Ŵavivye, dindi litondeke.
3 Mwize ‘mwe Thondwe la mlenji,
Mwize mtisanguruske;
M’samphanye mabibgu gha mdima,
M’paranye mifwiri ya nyifwa.
4 Mwize mlinda wa pa miyango,
Mutijurire vitumbiko;
Nthowa ya kuchanya munozge
Mujare yaku pharaniko.
5 Mwize ’mwe ŵa mphavu zose;
Imwe mukuwonya kutemwa
Pa mphinjika pa Kalvari,
Chiuta withu na Fumu.
