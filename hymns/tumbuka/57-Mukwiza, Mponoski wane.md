Mukwiza, Mponoski wane
William Henry Monk (1823-89)

1 Mukwiza, Mponoski wane,
mukwiza, Themba lane,
Mu kubuta, mu utozi,
m’uchindami wakuruska;
Tisamphuskenge sumu.
Kutema kwa mulenji
n’thenga la kwiza kwinu;
Mukwima Msofi wane;
m’liwo yinu yikwimba.
2 M’kwiza mwekha, m’kwiza nadi;
tamkumana mu nthowa,
Kumwonani, kum’manyani
kumpharirani vyose ’vyo
Tikapereka kale ndusumu uli ulo,
lwakutemwera Imwe!
Tamkwimba nakunozga,
na kumulambirani.
3 Mwe! nacho nchimwemwe,
Yesu, kumwonani na maso;
Ŵanthu ŵakumchindikani
pa chizumbe na kusopa,
Ŵakulamba na mtima!
Mwa Sekuru na Bwezi,
m’kuwoneka m’uweme;
Kose ’ko kuli munthu
ŵakuzomera Imwe.
