Pamoza na ng’ombe wakagona Yesu
William James Kirkpatrick (1838-1921)

1 Pamoza na ng’ombe wakagona Yesu;
Mu uryero wa ng’ombe wakawika mutu;
Nyenyezi yikaŵala, yikawona Yesu
Mu uteka mukati, wakugona tulo.
2 Kubama kwa ng’ombe kukamuchiruska,
Kweni Yesu mujedu ntha wakalira.
A Yesu, nkhumtemwani, mundilaŵiskenge,
Mukharenge na ine mpaka mlenji wize.
3 Muŵenge pafupi, mukhalenge nane,
Ndipo madazi ghose, fumu, munditemwe.
Ŵanichi ŵali wose muŵapwererere,
Tikharenge kuchanya pamoza na Imwe.
