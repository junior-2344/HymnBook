A Yesu, mukukhara
A.S.Sullivan | Wonaniso sumu 82

1 A Yesu, mukukhara
pa chizumbe kuchanya;
M’tilaŵiske na wezi;
m’tipulike Yesu.
2 Ta twana ntha tingopa
para tikumwonani;
M’kutemwa tose Khristu;
m’tipulike Yesu.
3 Ta twana timtemwani,
tipharazge kutemwa,
Timbenge sumu zithu;
m’tipulike Yesu.
4 Tumilimo tutowe,
tumitima tubute,
Ta twana tiŵe twinu;
m’tipulike Yesu.
5 Mutipokererenge
mu chipakato chinu,
Mumo titumbikike;
m’tipulike Yesu.
6 Yesu mwana wa Leza,
mukagona m’uryero,
M’kafwira pa mphinjika;
m’tipulike Yesu.
7 A Yesu na chizumbe,
mukusungira tose,
Tisuke tize kwinu;
m’tipulike Yesu
