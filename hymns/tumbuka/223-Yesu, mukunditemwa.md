Yesu, mukunditemwa
S.B. March

1 Yesu, mukunditemwa,
mu chipakato chinu
M’ndithaske ku vimphunga
na visuzgo vya mtima;
M’ndivikirire Yesu,
ku mayigha ghakofya;
Fiskani mtima wane
ku phota la kuchanya.
2 Mphonokwa ndilive 'mwe;
nkhukwapirana namwe;
Rekani kundisida;
m’ndikhozge, m’ndisangiske;
Nkhumugomezgani ’mwe;
movwiri wane ndimwe;
Mutu wane mudike papindo la uchizi.
3 Nkhukhumba Imwe pera;
Kundikhuska m’kuruska;
Nda m’techi, nda murwari
nda chinthuru, muchizge.
Ndimwe ŵatuŵa nadi,
ine ndiri muheni;
Wezi, nenesko nvyinu;
ine ndiri wa waka.
4 Mu na lusungu lose,
mungandigowokera;
Maji gha moyo ghize,
ghanditozge mu mtima;
Mbwiwi yenecho ndimwe;
ndimwengeko kwa Imwe.
Yibwibwituke mwane lero
na kunthazi swi.
