Nanga ndiŵe m’charu ichi ndiri mlendo
African Melody

1 Nanga ndiŵe m’charu ichi ndiri mlendo,
Charu chane ncha kuchanya chakutowa.
2 Aŵo ine nkhazgoŵera kuŵa naŵo,
Ndiŵo ŵali kundisida, mbakusangwa.
3 Ŵakurumba charu icho chakutowa,
Pakuti ncha mutende wenecho nadi.
4 Yesu Muponoski wane wali kuko,
Wakuromba kuti nane nkhafikeko.
5 Ine nane nkhulimbira charu icho,
Nkhaŵe na Yesu na ŵatuŵa wose swi.
6 Iwe wambura kumanya Yesu Mtaski,
Zgoka mtima kuti nawe ukanjire.
