Yesu ndalayizgana
Melchior Teschner, c. 1615

1 Yesu ndalayizgana
kum’limirani swi;
M’khare pafupi nane,
Sekuru na Bwezi.
Ntha tindopenge nkhondo
para muli nane.
Nesi kupuruka nthowa nga
mudangirepo.
2 Mukhare nane nadi,
vya m’charu viriko,
Vikundisomba m’maso
na m’makutu wuwo.
Walikoso ŵarwani kuwaro
na m’kati, ’Mwe Yesu
mundiphasizge ku viheni vyose.
3 Ndipulike kwa Imwe mazgu
ghakutefwa,
Ghatonde m’tafu wane
na kujitemwaso.
Mupe kundikhomeska,
kundendeska m’nthowa;
Ndimpulikirani Imwe,
Mlinda wa pa mtima.
4 Yesu m’kalayizgana
na mulimi yose
Wam’khara namwe uko,
m’kugadima kwinu;
Nane ndalayizgana
kum’limirani swi;
Ku mphaka za umoyo;
Sekuru m’ndovwire.
5 Ndiwone lwayo lwinu kuti
ndikandemo;
M’nkhongono zinu pera
nkhumurondezgani;
M’ndirongozgenge m’nthowa
ndisuke ndifike,
M’ndipokerere kuchanya,
Mponoski na Bwezi.
