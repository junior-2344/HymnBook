Kose uko kuli ŵanthu ŵimbe Leza Themba
Basil Harwood, 1859

1 Kose uko kuli ŵanthu ŵimbe Leza Themba.
M’machanya nkhutali, sumu zingafika;
Na pano pa charu rumbo likurenge;
Kose uko kuli ŵanthu ŵimbe Leza Themba.
2 Kose uko kuli ŵanthu ŵimbe Leza Themba
Ekleziya limbe, mtima ufwasenge;
Nawe mzimu wane umuchindikenge
Kose uko kuli ŵanthu ŵimbe Leza Themba.
