Kamphomo mkati mwa ŵangelo
Rutherford – F187, C581 | Passion chorale  - C 107, F182 (also as 377)

1. Kamphomo mkati mwa ŵangelo,
Kwababiwa Mponoski;
Nanga ŵa m’charu mbakudeka
Mwanangwa waŵizira.
Chorus
Kawone m’uryero, ndi Fumu
yirimo;
Kawone m’uryero, Chiuta ndi
munthu
2. Ŵakutayika mbakudeka
Chimwemwe mu ŵangelo,
Chiuta ndi Imanueli,
Wali pamo na ŵanthu.
3. Pulikani sumu ya mutende,
Yakwimbika m’chimwemwe
Sangwani pamo na ŵangelo,
Urumbenge Chiuta.
4. Mu nyumba za Ufumunkhuru
Ntha umusangengemo
Iyo wavura uchindami
Kanjire umo wali.
5. Chiuta wajizgora munthu:
Icho nchakutondweska;
Chiuta wafwa pa Kalvari,
Charuska kutondweska.
