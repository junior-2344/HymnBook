Mtima wane, ŵanyane
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Mtima wane, ŵanyane
wimbirenge Fumu yane,
Kumtamika Yehova Fumu
yaneya lusungu.
Ndimbe, ndimbe,
ndimwimbirenge Mponoski;
Ndimbe, ndimbe,
waponoska moyo wane.
2 Viyuni vyamtamika,
ŵanthu ŵakukhara chete;
Milimo yake yose
yikumwimbira Mponoski.
3 Mulenji na muhanya,
mise na usiku wuwo,
Ndimkumbuke Yehova,
wakundikumbuka ine.
4 Mphepo, Nyanja, majigha
vyatamika Fumu yane;
Nane ndirondorenge
kumtamika Fumu yane.
5 Ŵangelo na ŵatuŵa
ŵakumtamika kuchanya;
Mtuŵa, mtuŵa, mwe Mtuŵa;
Mtuŵa, Aleluya Amen.
