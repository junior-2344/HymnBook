’Mwe Yesu mwimirira
Samuel Sebastian Wesley (1810-76)

1 ’Mwe Yesu mwimirira
ku nyumba kuwaro,
Ku mtima wakujarwa;
mwakhumba kunjira
Tiŵe na soni kuti tiri
kwamba kale Kumupani
chitima nakum’jarirani.
2 ’Mwe Yesu m’kukhung’uska,
woko linu bamba;
Pa mutu winu minga,
mu maso masozi;
Ehe! Nkhutemwa uli,
kwakuzizipizga!
Nkhuheni kwakuruska kuzina
Mtaski ’yo!
3 ’Mwe Yesu mukuweya,
makora, zikiti:
“Nkhafwira iwe kale,
Kasi wandikana?”
Sono Fumu na soni
nkhujura muryango;
Munjire, Mtaski wane,
mukharengemo swi.
