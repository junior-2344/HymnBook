Tandazgani mazgu
Wm. J. Kirkpatrick

1 Tandazgani mazgu
uko kuli munthu,
Uko kose kuli wa mtima uheni;
Uyo ndi Mukhristu
wapharazge kuti
Msanguruski wiza.
Msanguruski wiza, Msanguruski wiza,
Mzimu wa kuchanya, Pangano la Wiske
Tandazgani mazgu uko kuli munthu,
Msanguruski wiza.
2 Usikuwaruta, muhanya wafika,
Wamarizga chitengero
chakofya ‘mwe;
Mapiri ghaŵara
na muhanya uwu
Msanguruski wiza.
3 Themba la nkhongono
la mathemba ghose,
Likuwombola mauzima
gha ŵanthu;
Ntheura tikwimba sumu na kupinga;
Msanguruski wiza.
4 Ndite uli ine pakuŵandurira
Ŵabali ŵanyane na kutemwa uku;
Yesu wakumanya kutitemwa nadi
Msanguruski wiza.
5 Tiye timwimbire Muponoski withu,
Lusumu lwa kutemwa kwake
kwa ndanda;
Ŵatuŵa ŵakuchanya ŵapokerere
Msanguruski wiza.
