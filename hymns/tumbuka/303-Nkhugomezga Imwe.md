Nkhugomezga Imwe
Lowell Mason 1792-1872

1 Nkhugomezga Imwe
’Mwe ŵa pa Kalvari;
Muponoski;
M’pulike nkhuromba,
Mufumye uheni,
Kufuma ku lero
Ndiŵe winu.
2 Mundipe uchizi,
Undikhomeskenge,
M’ndipamphuske;
Umo m’kandifwira,
Ndimo kuŵirenge
Kutemweska kwane
Nga ndi moto.
3 Ndingenda m’chinkhamwa
Vitima vyandane,
M’ndirongozge;
Chisi m’zgore mhanya;
Mufyule masozi,
Ndireke kuzgeŵa
M’nthowa yinu.
4 Pa kumara pano,
Muronga wa nyifwa
Ndimirwemo,
‘Mwe’ Mthaski, m’lusungu;
Muwuskeko wofi,
M’ndiyeghere kwinu,
Nda ’kuphokwa.
