Yesu ndiyo Themba; mumlambirenge ’mwe
John Darwall (1731-89)

1 Yesu ndiyo Themba; mumlambirenge ’mwe;
Mwa ŵanthu imbani, mupingenge ruta,
M’kwezge nkhumbi, mkwezge mazgu,
Muchenge, nkhuti muchenge.
2 Yesu Muponoski ndiyo wakuwusa;
Ndiyo watithaska; wali pa chizumbe;
M’kwezge nkhumbi, m’kwezge mazgu,
Muchenge nkhuti muchenge.
3 Ufumu wa Yesu ntha ungatondeka;
Na chofyo cha nyifwa ntha chiriko lero;
M’kwezge nkhumbi, m’kwezge mazgu,
Muchenge, nkhuti muchenge.
4 Wali pachizumbe ca wiske kuchanya;
Wakulindirira tisuke tilambe;
M’kwezge nkhumbi, m’kwezge mazgu,
Muchenge nkhuti muchenge.
5 Chigomezgo chithu: Mweruzgi wakwiza;
Wamkutipokera ku kaya la ndanda
Tapulika m’ngelo m’kuru,
Mbata yamkuti, muchenge.
