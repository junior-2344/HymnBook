Munthu wa chitima, ’mwe!
Phillip Bliss (1838-76)

1 Munthu wa chitima, ’mwe!
Ndi mwana wa Chiuta.
Wakiza katifwira;
Aleluya, ndi Mponoski!
2 Wakayegha vitima;
wakanderuzgikira;
Wakandiwombolaso;
Aleluya, ndi Mponoski!
3 Wakayegha uheni!
Uyo ngwambura banga;
Wakapemanya nadi;
Aleluya, ndi Mponoski!
4 Wakapayikika ’mwe!
Wakachema: “Kwamara,”
Ntheura wakaruska;
Aleluya, ndi Mponoski!
5 Themba la uchindami
lizamkutitorera
Uko tamkwimba sumu:
Aleluya, ndi Mponoski
