Ŵadada tiri pano sono
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Ŵadada tiri pano sono,
Tawungana panthazi pinu.
2 Zaninge mukati mwa ise,
Muyowoye nase ŵadada.
3 Para tikuromba’mpulike,
Mutipe vitumbiko vyinu.
4 Mutumemazgungandimoto,
Ghachitenge m’kati mwa ise.
5 Gumanyani mitima yithu,
Na Muponoski withu Yesu.
6 Chiponosko chinu chiwuke,
Mwa ise na Yesu Muponoski.
7 Mazgu ghinu ghose ghaŵenge
Ghakuchizga mizimu yithu.
8 Taŵeya Ŵadada m’tumbike
Mpingo wose wongana pano.
