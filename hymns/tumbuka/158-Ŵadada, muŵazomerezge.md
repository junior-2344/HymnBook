Ŵadada, muŵazomerezge
African Melody

1 Ŵadada, muŵazomerezge
aŵa ŵima panthazi pinu,
Zomerani muŵapokere,
muzomere ŵaŵe ŵanthu ŵinu nadi
2 A Yesu, awo ŵanthu ŵinu,
mukafwira laiŵawombola,
Mbinu lero. Awo ŵakuti,
tikuŵika Imwe mu mitima yithu.
3 Na Imwe A Mzimu Mutuŵa,
mwize sono,mukhare nawo
Muwovwire kwenda makora;
muzuzge mitima yawo na chimango
4 Ŵachite milimo yiweme,
muwapenge wose nkhongono,
Kuti ŵende kwakunyoroka,
ŵasuke ŵafike kwa imwe kuchanya
5 Ŵadada, Imwe m’tumbikike,
namwe Yesu, Mponoski withu,
Na A Mzimu muwangweruske,
Chiuta yumoza wakuzgike nadi.
