’Mwe Yehova wa lusungu
Orlando Gibbons (1583-1625) | Salmo 51 (Wonaniso Sumu 117)

1 ’Mwe Yehova wa lusungu,
m’ndirongore wezi;
Mufumye uheni wane,
nkhumurombani ’mwe.
2 Nda wakwananga ndaŵeya
mundigowokere;
Nda wakufipirwa m’savye;
m’ndisuke nda mheni.
3 Ndimwe ndamunangirani, ’mwe
Yehova mkuru;
mrandu wanditonda nadi, wa
Mweruzgi wane!
4 Hena! Imwe m’kusekera uvwe ndarunjika;
M’ndipe vinjeru, vikhare
ku mutima kusi.
5 Mungandifumyanga kwinu,
m’ngafumyanga Mzimu;
M’ndiwezgerenge chimwemwe,
M’ndikhozgenge Imwe.
6 Ipo nane ndamsekera,
nda wa mtima upya,
Uvwe Imwe mwalengera
mzimu wakukhoma.
