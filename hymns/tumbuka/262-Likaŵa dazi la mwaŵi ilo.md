Likaŵa dazi la mwaŵi ilo
P. Dodridge

1 Likaŵa dazi la mwaŵi ilo
nkhamsangira Mtaski,
Ndipo nkhusekera m’mtima
pakuphara kucha kwane
Dazi’lo, dazi’lo Yesu wakandisavyira!
Wakasambizga kuromba,
na kucha mazuwa ghose;
Dazi’lo, dazi’lo Yesu wakandisavyira.
2 Nkhapangana nayo Yesu:
ndiri wake nayo ngwane;
Wakachema nkhamrondezga,
nkhasekera kumzomera.
3 Wa mtima wane pumula
m’chikhazi cha Fumu yako;
Urekenge kuwukamo, una
viweme mwa Iyo.
4 Mwa wakaboni kuchanya,
Ndindipangane nayo swi;
Ndirumbenge pangano ’lo.
Ndisuke ndifwirengemo.
