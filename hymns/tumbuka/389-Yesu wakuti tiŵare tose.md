Yesu wakuti tiŵare tose
Edward Arthur (1874-1948)

1 Yesu wakuti tiŵare tose
Tiŵe nga ndi nyali pa usiku bi,
Mdima uli m’charu tiŵarenge
Iwe nawe kwako, nane kwane.
2 Yesu wakuti timuŵarire
Mwene wakumanya vyose tachita,
Wakundilaŵiska umo ndiriri
Iwe nawe kwako, nane kwane.
3 Yesu wakuti tiwovwirenge
Wose ŵa chitima, wose ŵatechi,
Tiŵasanguruske, tiŵarenge
Iwe nawe kwako, nane kwane.
