Pano nkhumuwonani Fumu yane
James Langran (1835-1909)

1 Pano nkhumuwonani Fumu yane;
Pano kukhwaska vya mu chindindi;
Pano nkhu kora uchizi wa swi,
Nda wakuvuka nkhweghama kwinu.
2 Pano ndirye chibama cha Leza;
Pano namwe ndimwe vya kuchanya;
Pano nditaye mphingo za pasi,
Pano ndiche: ndagowokereka.
3 Ndi nyengo ya monesko na sumu;
Vyose ivi vyandinozgekera;
Pano ndiryenge, ndirutirizge.
Nyengo ’yi yakukumana namwe.
4 Vimanyiro vikumara sono;
Kutemwa kwinu ntha kukuruta,
Chibama, Mpheska, vikusezgeka;
’Mwe, Nguru yane, muchali nane.
5 Ndirive wakuvwira munyake;
Pa woko linu ndipo ndeghame.
Ndipera, Fumu yane, ndipera,
Lusoko lwane ndi mphavu zinu.
6 Ngwane uheni, ngwinu urunji;
Nkhwane kwananga, nkhwinu kusinga,
Ndimo muli mphanji na chimango
M’ndopa zinu na urunji winu.
7 Sangano na sangano likwiza,
Kutirazgira ku la kuchanya
Ghakutipukwiska kuya uko
Ku monesko ukuru wa Fumu.
