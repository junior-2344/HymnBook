Nkhusangwa na mise yino
Johann Georg Ebeling (1637-76)

1 Nkhusangwa na mise yino,
Kose kose mazgu ghakupulikikwa;
Yesu wawa, ndiyo nyimbo,
Mu charu, mu mtambo,
chimwemwe chirimo.
2 Pulikani Thenga ilo,
M’uryero likuti, chimbirani wofi;
Zaninge muwomboleke
Ku suzgo lose la usokwano winu.
3 Tiyeni, tirute uko,
ŵakuru, ŵachoko
ŵamulambirenge;
Timtemwe, wakutitemwa,
Yesu ndi nyenyezi
yakutirongozga.
4 Fumu, ndimwe wakuzirwa,
Ndimteŵeterani sono
na muyaya;
Na kuchanya, pamo namwe
Ndiŵe na chimwemwe
chambura kumara.
