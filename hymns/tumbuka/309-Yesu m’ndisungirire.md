Yesu m’ndisungirire
W.D. Doane

1 Yesu m’ndisungirire
ku mphinjika yinu;
Uko kukufumira mronga
wakuchizga.
M’mphinjika m'mphinjika,
muŵe uchindami,
Ndisuke ndifikenge m’charu cha kusirya.
2 Nda munthu wa chitenthe
nkhasangamo wezi;
Nyenyezi ya mulenji
yikandiŵalira.
3 Mu mphinjika,
A Yesu, Mwana wa Chiuta;
M’ndovwire ndendengemo
mu mufwiri wake.
4 Ndilindirire mumo,
kugomezga ruta.
Ndisuke ndifikemo m’charu
cha Kusirya
