Nkhuzomera fumu yane
Thomas Jackson (1715-81) | Wonaniso S&S 883

1 Nkhuzomera fumu yane,
ndiri na soni cha;
Nkhurumba wezi wa Yesu,
nkhumchindika Iyo.
2 Yesu Fumu nkhumumanya,
nkhujitumbirapo;
Wizamruwa ine chara,
nanga nkhunditaya.
3 Nkhujipereka kwa Yesu,
Iyo wakumanya;
Kundisungirira wengu
madazi ghose swi.
4 Zina lane wizamchema
panthazi pa Wiske;
Nditi ndinjire mu muzi wake
wa muyaya.
