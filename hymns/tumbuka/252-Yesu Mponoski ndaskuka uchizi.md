Yesu Mponoski ndaskuka uchizi
Moses Shumba

1 Yesu Mponoski ndaskuka uchizi,
Wenewuwo mwandirongora ine.
Mwandinjizga mu chiponosko,
Mwandisuka ku zakwananga.
2 Mu ujira na mu uheni wane
Mukiza kandifumiskamo nadi.
3 Ine nkharatanga ku pharaniko,
Mu na mabamba chifukwa cha ine.
4 Nkhuzizwa mukutemwa nda muheni,
Mwakuti mundinyoroske nkhanira.
5 Ndilive chakumuwongerani ’mwe,
Nkhujipereka ndamwene kwa Imwe.
