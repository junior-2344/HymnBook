Wuka iwe, Mkhristu, kozga njasko yako
Rev. C.C. Chinula

1 Wuka iwe, Mkhristu, kozga njasko yako,
Lika m’nyumba yako, na kuŵanthu wose.
2 Kozga njasko, Mkhristu, likam’kaya lako,
Ŵa mu m’dima nawo mungweruke.
3 Kozga njasko, Mkhristu, lika kose kose;
Na m’mitundu wuwo, namo mungweruke.
4 Kutemwa kwa Yesu kukuzingizgenge;
Nawe tutuzgika na kutemwa uku.
5 Mazgu gha Chiuta ndi njasko yikuru,
Yigolere mwako kwambura kuzima.
6 Njasko yako, Mkhristu, yikozgeke ruta,
Mu mutima wako na Mzimu mutuŵa.
