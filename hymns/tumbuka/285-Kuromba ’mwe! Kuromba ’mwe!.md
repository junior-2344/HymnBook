Kuromba ’mwe! Kuromba ’mwe!
W.B. Bradbury

1 Kuromba ’mwe! Kuromba ’mwe!
Kukuruwiska vya m’charu;
Kukunditorera kwinu,
Vya ku mtima ndikuphara;
Na mu nyengo za vitima
Mzimu wane wavwirika,
Wataskika ku urwani,
Pa kwiza kwinu m’kuromba.
2 Mu kuromba, mu kuromba,
Nkhufwatulira vya mtima;
Uyo ngwakugomezgeka
Kovwira mzimu uranda;
Ndipo apa wandichema
Kugomezga mazgu ghakhe,
Nditi ndilaŵiske kwake
Na kulindizga m’kuromba.
3 Mukuromba, mu kuromba,
Ndimo ndisangurukire,
Mpaka ndiŵe m’chipumulo
Na kudukira kukaya
Pa kuvura thupi ili;
Ndampokera sekeresko,
Marombo sono ghamara,
Nkhuwona chisko cha Fumu.
