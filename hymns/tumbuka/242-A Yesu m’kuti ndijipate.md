A Yesu m’kuti ndijipate
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 A Yesu m’kuti ndijipate;
mundingweruske ndijipate;
Nthowa yandisoŵa vyalema,
Yesu m’ndovwire ndijipate.
Chorus
Ndawuka A Yesu m’ndovwirenge
Pakumurondezgani mu nthowa,
Ndimurondani rondo-rondo,
Yesu, m’ndovwire ndijipate.
2 A Yesu, m’kuti ndijipate,
ndiri mjira kujikanizga,
Yesu m’ndovwire kujipata,
ndimanye kwenda m’nthowa yinu.
3 A Yesu m’kuti ndijipate,
Jurani mtima ndijipate
M’ndisambizgenge kujipata,
ndimanye kuyegha mphinjika.
