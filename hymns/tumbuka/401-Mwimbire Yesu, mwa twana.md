Mwimbire Yesu, mwa twana
Adapted from Handel (1685-1759)

1 Mwimbire Yesu, mwa twana,
Wakumulawiskani.
Mwimbire kutemwa kwake,
Na lusungu lukuru.
Timchindike, timchindike,
Yesu Fumu yikuru;
Timchindike, timchindike,
Fumu yithu yikuru.
2 Apa wakikhira pasi,
Na kukhara na ŵanthu,
Ŵana ŵakamtamikanga,
Ndipo wakasekera.
3 Ŵanyina ŵakiza kwake,
Wakamyegliera Iwana,
Yesu wakatupakata,
Na kutumbika ito.
4 Kura kuchanya nakoso,
Ŵangelo ŵakumrumba;
Ŵanandi-nadi ŵalipo,
Ŵakumwimbira kuko.
5 Mwimbire Yesu, mwa twana,
M’bali winu muweme,
Musuke mukamwonenge,
Mukamtamikenge swi.
