Uyu Yesu, uyu Yesu wiza mu mutima wane
Rev. J.P. Chirwa

1 Uyu Yesu, uyu Yesu wiza mu mutima wane;
Viheni vyose vifume,Yesu yekha wakharemo;
Mtima wane ndapereka kwa Yesu Mponoski wane.
2 Uyu Yesu, uyu Yesu wiza mu mutima wane;
Sono wafumye Satana muno mu mutima wane;
Sono vyose ndapereka kwa Yesu Mponoski wane.
3 Wakimirira, wakakhung’uska ku mutima wane;
Na mawoko agho ghakakhomeka pa champhinjika;
Nkhiza, nkhamjurira nkhati, “Njiraninge, Fumu yane.”
