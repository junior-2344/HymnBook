Sekererani! Muwomboli wiza
Darwall – C1 35, F202 | Wesley - C76, F536

1 Sekererani! Muwomboli wiza;
Wonani uryero, mphinga na dindi.
Mtumbikani pharanani wakatifwira,
Mtumbikani, sekerani, ngwa uzimaso.
2 Sekererani! Dazi likuŵara;
Mabingu gharuta, chisi chamara.
3 Sekererani! Tiri kuthaskika;
Muweme wakafwira taŵaheni.
4 Sekererani! Mwana-mberere ’yo
Watonda nyifwa, ngwa uzimaso.
5 Sekererani! papo Themba lithu
Liri kuchanya kutiphepeskera.
6 Sekererani! Liti lizengeso,
Kwiza mu uchindami ’lo likafwa.
