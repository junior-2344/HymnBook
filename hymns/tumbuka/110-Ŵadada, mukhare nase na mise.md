Ŵadada, mukhare nase na mise
Bayeux Church Melody

1 Ŵadada, mukhare nase na mise;
Ndimwe Mwenecho wa m’hanya na chisi;
Tikujipa kwinu, mutipumuzge, M’tivikirire.
2 Maghanoghano ghaheni ghazgeŵe;
Kusuke kuche mutisungirire; M’thupi na m’zimu
mutirongozgenge, M’tume ŵangelo.
3 Timulangulukireni mu tulo,
Imweso muŵe pa kuwuka kwithu
Tizamkuteŵetera dazi lose Na kum’rumbani.
4 Tilive mphanji pano, kweni kwinu;
Mutitaske Dada, ndimwe mwenecho;
Ntha mungajowora ta ŵakusokwa,
Ta ŵanthu ŵinu.
5 Dada, zina linu lichindikike,
Khumbo linu lichitike na ŵanthu;
M’tisungirire, mutigowokere sono na myaya.
