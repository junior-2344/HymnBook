Mwati wa Ekleziya ndi Yesu Mponoski
Samuel Sebastian Wesley (1810-76)

1 Mwati wa Ekleziya ndi Yesu Mponoski;
Ndiyo mafigha ghake ghakukhaliraswi;
Ndiyo musweni wake, wakikha kuchanya
Kwiza kaliwombola na kulifwiraso.
2 Yesu Fumu wasora mu mafuko ghose
Ŵakutemweka ŵake ŵakumugomezga;
Wose ŵakuchindika Fumu yimoza’yo,
Wose ŵakuwungana pa gome limoza.
3 Ŵanthu ŵakuhoyera na kuliyuyura
Ekleziya lichitechi lakusuzgika;
Kweni m’chizizipizgo ŵalinda Ŵakhristu
Wakucherezga mpaka dazi lichenge che!
4 Nanga likulimbana mukurwa kwakofya,
Kweni likulaŵira mutende kuchanya:
Mpaka dazi liŵare na kuruska kwake,
Dazi la kupumula na Karonga wake.
5 Na sono pano pasi Ekleziya ili
Liriso na sangano na Chiuta wake;
Mu wezi winu, Fumu, mutizomerezgenge
Kukhara namwe kuko muyirayira swi.
