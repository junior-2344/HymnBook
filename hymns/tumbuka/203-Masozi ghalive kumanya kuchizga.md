Masozi ghalive kumanya kuchizga
Rev. R. Lowry

1 Masozi ghalive kumanya kuchizga,
Na kuwuskapo chofyo, na kundisuka uheni,
Ntha ghandandichizga.
Yesu wakandifwira,wakafwira pa khuni,
Wakulindizga ine kuti wandichizge.
2 Mulimo ulivye kumanya kundichizga,
Nanga uŵe uweme, ntha ungandiponoska,
Ntha ungandichizga.
3 Chizitu chilivye kumanya kundichizga,
Yesu wakundichema, m’chizitu ndindifwenge,
Ntha chingandichizga.
4 Ndim’zomere Yesu, ndiyo wangandichizga,
Ndipulikane nayo,Yesu m’ndovwire ine,
Kuti ndichizgike.
