M’ndibise, Mponoski wane
W.D.Doane

1 M’ndibise, Mponoski wane,
mu nyumba yinu;
Ndipumule m’uchindami,
ndiwonenge Imwe.
Mundibise, A Mponoski, m’ndibise!
Yesu Mponoski, ndikhare na Imwe.
2 Ku vimphupuru m’ndibise;
ndingamirwanga,
M’maŵiruŵiru gha Nyanja,
ndidukire kwinu.
3 M’dibise para mutima
uli mu suzgo;
M’ndisangwiske mu masozi;
mungandisidanga.
