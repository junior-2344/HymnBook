Yesu wakuchema tose ku
Christian Friedrich Witt (1660-1716)

1 Yesu wakuchema tose ku
visuzgo vya m’charu,
Nyengo zose wakuchema.
“Mkhristu we ndirondezge.”
2 Nga nkhu Nyanja ŵapositoli
kale ŵakapulika,
Ŵakendakareka vyose
ku m’rondezga m’kutemwa.
3 Wakutichema tireke
kusopaso vya waka,
Ivyo vikupuvya mtima,
kuti “Mkhristu, nditemwa.”
4 Mukukondwa, mu kulira,
mu m’limo, mu pumulo,
Wakuchema-chema.
kuti, “Mwaŵanthu nditemwani”.
5 Yesu, Mthaski mwatichema
pa lusungu lukuru;
Tapulika; timtemwani,
na kumlimirani swi.
