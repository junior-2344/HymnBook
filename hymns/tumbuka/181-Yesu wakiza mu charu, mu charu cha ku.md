Yesu wakiza mu charu, mu charu cha ku
Galileya | Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Yesu wakiza mu charu, mu charu cha ku Galileya;
Wakasambizganga ŵanthu kuti ŵazgokenge ku mtima.
Wakuti, “Zgokanga m’mtima.”
Yesu wati, zgokanga m’mtima” (kaŵiri)
2 Ŵanji vvakumurondezga, para ŵapulika mazgu;
Wakaŵachema nakuti, “ŵanthu ŵazgokenge ku mtima”
3 Wachali kuchema nadi, kuti ŵanthu ŵazgoke m’mtima,
Mazgu ghake ghakuchema, lusi-lusi m’nyumba na m’thondo.
4 Yesu wakutemwa wose, ruta-ruta wakuŵachema,
Mazgu ghake ghakupulikikwa, agho wakayowoya kale.
5 Sono wali pa muryango, nakuyowoya mazgu agho;
Wakujulizga mitima, kuti ŵajureko wanjire.
