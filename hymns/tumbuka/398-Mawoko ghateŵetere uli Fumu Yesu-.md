Mawoko ghateŵetere uli Fumu Yesu?
Henry Elliot Button (1861-1925)

1 Mawoko ghateŵetere uli Fumu Yesu?
Ghalimenge tumilimo
twakovwirira ŵakavu,
Ndimo ghachitire.
2 Mulomo uteŵetere uli Fumu Yesu?
Uchindike na kuromba,
uphare lusungu pera,
Ndimo uchitire.
3 Na maso ghateŵetere uli Fumu Yesu?
Ghasambire Buku lake,
gharazge lusi kuchanya,
Ndimo ghachitire.
4 Mutima uteŵetere uli Fumu Yesu?
Mutima umutemwenge
Mubali na Mtaski wake,
Ndimo uchitire.
