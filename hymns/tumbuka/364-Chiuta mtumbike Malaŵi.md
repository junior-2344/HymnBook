Chiuta mtumbike Malaŵi
The National Anthem

1 Chiuta mtumbike Malaŵi,
Mumupe mutende.
Muthereske ŵarwani ŵose,
Njara, nthenda, sanje.
Gumanyani mitima yithu,
Chofyo tiŵevyeso
Mtumbike mlongozgi na tose,
Na Mama Malaŵi.
2 Malaŵi charu chakutowa,
Nyata na wanangwa.
Nyanja, mphepo za mumapiri
Taŵamwaŵi ndise,
M’madambo nyata ya vundira
Vyatipa wanangwa.
Nguyi na madambo ghaweme,
Kutowa Malaŵi.
3 Wanangwa muyaya pamoza,
Tizenge Malaŵi.
Kutemwa na mwamphu na nchindi,
Timuteŵetere.
Mnyengo ya nkhondo nesi mtende,
Chinkhomo chimoza.
Mama, dada, mwe tijipate,
Mkukuzga Malaŵi.
