Imwe Yehova mwandipachira
African Melody | Salmo 139

1 Imwe Yehova mwandipachira,
Imwe Yehova mwandipachira,
Mukundimanya.
2 Mukumanyaso kugona kwane,
Mukumanyaso kugona kwane,
Na kwenda kwane.
3 Mukulindaso kukhara kwane,
Mukulindaso kukhara kwane,
Na kawukiro.
4 Mukumanya na mutima wane,
Mukumanya na mtima wane
Uli kutali.
5 Kweni mulije kanthu kaweme,
Kweni mulije kanthu kaweme
Mu mlomo wane.
6 Mzimu Mutuŵa wize mumtima,
Mzimu Mutuŵa wize mumtima,
Wandisambizge.
7 Kuti ndimanye lusungu lwinu,
Kuti ndimanye lusungu lwinu
Ulo mwandipa.
