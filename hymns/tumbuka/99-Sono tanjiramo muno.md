Sono tanjiramo muno
Rev. P.Z. Thole

1 Sono tanjiramo muno
m’nyumba yino Fumu,
Mazgu ghakupulikikwa kuti,
“Khara chete.”
2 Pa kongana m’nyumba yake
Chiuta wafika,
Nakuyowoya ku wose kuti,
“Khara chete.”
3 Nchimwemwe mu mtima wa
munthu wa ku Yehova
Pakupulika mazgu ghakuti,
“Khara chete.”
4 Sekererani tiri m’nyumba
yake sono imwe
Kuwungana naye pamo wati,
“Khara chete.”
5 Pakupulika mazgu gha
Yehova sono,
Yehova wayowoyenge wati,
“Khara chete”.
6 Mitima ya ŵanthu ŵake
yawungana sono,
Kumlaŵiska na kumlinda wati,
“Khara chete.”
7 Yowoyani, ndimwe Fumu
yithu, tipulike,
Tihenere, tisekere,
nakukhara chete.
