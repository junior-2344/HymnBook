Ŵa mwaŵi mbatuŵa
Spiess's Gesangbuch, Heidelberg, 1745

1 Ŵa mwaŵi mbatuŵa;
ŵamwona Chiuta;
Msambizgi wawo ndi Yesu,
wakukhara nawo.
2 Fumu yikikhira, yikiza yamoyo;
Yazgoka munthu nga ndise;
yatipa bambiro.
3 Ku ŵakuyuyuka yikujipa ruta;
Chikhazi chake na msumba
mbatuŵa mu mtima.
4 Tikumukhumbani,
Yesu m’titumbike;
M’tipe mtima wakupera,
wakuyana namwe.
