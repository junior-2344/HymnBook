Yesu pa monesko ’wu
MS. Dresdan, 1761

1 Yesu pa monesko ’wu,
mtima wose muliske,
Na chibama cha moyo.
2 Mpheska tikuzicheta,
chimanyiro chandopa,
M’tizuzge na kutemwa.
3 Pakuwona mphinjika
tikulira uheni;
Fumu, m’tisanguruske.
4 Mtiguzire m’luŵavu umo,
m’kafuma ndopa;
Tibisemo kwananga.
5 Mtisutule m’kwananga,
m’kuzge chipulikano;
Fumu, mutipe mtende.
6 Na kawoko kwa bamba
m’teteske, m’mtifikiske
M’charu chinu chiweme.
