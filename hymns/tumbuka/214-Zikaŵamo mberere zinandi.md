Zikaŵamo mberere zinandi
Ira. D. Sankey

1 Zikaŵamo mberere zinandi
Mu lukutu ku kaya;
Kweni yimoza yikazgewapo,
Yekha kumapopa yi;
Kutali na mliska muweme,
Kutali na kaya yiweme,
Kutali na mliska muweme.
2 "Fumu mberere nzinandi,
Lukutu lwazura fwa,
Tikuzizwa kuti mukupenja
Yimoza, maso kwa."
Fumu yikazgora, "Yene ’yo
Yiri kuzgeŵera m’mayiyi;
Ndirute sono nkhayipenje.”
3 Na yumo munthu wamanya
Kofya kwa mironga,
Na chisi cha usiku uwo,
Icho wakendamo
Mu mayiyi wakayisanga
Yikarwara na kuzirika,
Yikuberereskera mu nyifwa.
4 “Fumu, ndopa za mu nthowa
Zasururirachi?”
“Zathikira wakupuruka
Kuti wawereko.”
“Mawoko ghaparuka uli?”
“Ghakarasika na usiku
Mu minga yinandi – nandi.”
5 Mu mapiri na m’thengere wuwo
Ehe! Yapulikikwa
Nchemerezgo kuti,
“Kondwani,
Ndayisanga mberere.”
Ŵangelo wose kuchanya
Ŵazgora, sekeraninge ’mwe”
Fumu yawera na mberere.”
