Nda wakwananga ndapona
Rev. C.C. Chinula

1 Nda wakwananga ndapona,
goliwoli lasutuka,
Landifwenyanga m’singo ’mo,
Yesu wakandiwombola.
Ndasutuka, ndawombokwa,
Yesu wakhala mkati' mo,
Wakuya nane m‘Zion, watuŵa wakuhenera.
2 Vikondo vya murwani ’mwe pafupi kundimarizga.
Ndamwene na nyumba yane, Yesu wakandiwombola.
3 Pa kusutulika kwane ŵangelo ŵalulutira,
Ŵahemba ŵandivulira vya pa mtima vya ku Yesu.
4 M’Yerusalemu kuchanya mphomo yikapulikikwa,
Ŵangelo ŵatuŵa wose ŵakundihenera nadi.
