Fumu, masika ghacha pa dambo na phiri
Mainz Gesangbuch, c. 1833

1 Fumu, masika ghacha pa dambo na phiri;
Ŵangelo ŵizamvuna mphundwe zakuzirwa;
Mupokerere ŵapya ŵateŵeti ŵinu,
Wafiske khumbo linu mu ŵanthu ŵa pasi.
2 Munda wa mpheska ngwinu, muŵatumizgeko
Nanga mba kuthukira tiŵachenge wuwo;
Ntha ŵakupenja njombe, mbakovwira waka;
Mba mlimo pamo namwe, ndiko nkhucha kwawo.
3 Muŵe nawo ŵadada, namwe Yesu fumu,
Na Imwe Mzimu Mtuŵa, yumoza m’utatu;
Ŵaŵe ŵasopi nadi, ŵamsopani Imwe,
Wazure wezi winu lero na m’ruta swi.
