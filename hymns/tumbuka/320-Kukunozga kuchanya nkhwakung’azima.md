Kukunozga kuchanya nkhwakung’azima
Anon.

1 Kukunozga kuchanya nkhwakung’azima;
Awo ŵali kuyako ŵakusekera,
VVakukhara chikhazi ntha wakusunkhunika,
Ŵali na chimango swi, wakucha swi-swi.
Chimwemwe! Chimwemwe!
Awo ŵakutemwana, reka ŵakucha.
2 Fumu yithu Chiuta yikusekera
Pakuwona ŵakristu ŵakutemwana;
Pakuti ŵakuchanya iŵo ŵakutemwana,
Kutemwana ndi dango la Fumu yithu.
3 Dango lipya nkhumpani kuti m’temwane,
Kuti ŵanthu ŵamanye muli ŵa ine;
Uvwe mundatemwane ŵanthu ŵakuzukuma,
Para mukutinkhana mwa ŵanthu ŵane.
