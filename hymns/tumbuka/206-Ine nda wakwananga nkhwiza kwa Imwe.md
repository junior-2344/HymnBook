Ine nda wakwananga nkhwiza kwa Imwe
Y. Khonje

1 Ine nda wakwananga nkhwiza kwa Imwe
Fumu mundijurirenge nkwiza kwa Imwe.
Nkwiza Fumu, nkwiza Fumu,
Nkwiza na kumupenjani, nkwiza kwa Imwe.
2 Nanga nkhwenda pa charu ndusungu lwinu
Usange mukundisida nditi ndifwenge.
3 Chipulikano cha Dada Abrahamu
Chikundigoska nadi nda wakwanangika.
4 Ndachimbira Mponoski wane wa ndanda,
Nkhujikoma ndekha nadi nkhujimarizga.
