Nkhasekereranga para ŵakati, tirute
Adapted from Christopher Tye (1508-72) | Salmo 122

1 Nkhasekereranga para ŵakati, tirute
Tose kwa Chiuta withu, ndiyo tikamsope.
2 ’Mwe malundi ghithu nadi ghizamkwimilira
Mu nyumba yake yiweme yakusoperamo.
3 Ndiko mafuko gha ŵanthu ghakuya kawonga
Fumu yawo Muponoski, nase tikawonge.
4 Ndiko chiriko chizumbe cha Fumu yikuru;
Yikupokerera ŵanthu; Tikayilambire.
5 Tikurombera mipingo ya ŵanyithu wose;
Tikuti ŵaŵe mu mtende, mtende wa Chiuta.
6 Chimango chiŵenge mose mu vipata vyawo;
Uchizgi nawo na wezi wa Fumu ya tose.
7 Ŵanyithu ŵa kosekose ŵatumbikikenge;
Mpingo wa ŵakutemwana ukharirire swi.
