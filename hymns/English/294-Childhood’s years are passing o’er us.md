Childhood’s years are passing o’er us
294       Sicilian Mariners___

1 Childhood’s years are passing o’er us;
Soon our school-days will be done;
Cares and sorrows lie before us,
Hidden dangers, snares unknown.
2 O may He who, meek and lowly,
Trod Himself this vale of woe
Make us His, and make us holy, Guard and guide us while we go.
3 Hark! it is the Saviour calling, “Little children, follow Me!”
Jesus, keep our feet from falling; Teach us all to follow Thee.
4 Soon we part-it may be never, Never here to meet again;
O to meet in heav’n for ever! O the crown of life to gain!
