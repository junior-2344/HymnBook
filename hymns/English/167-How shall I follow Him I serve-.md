How shall I follow Him I serve?
167    Hesperus (Elim)___C501,

1 How shall I follow Him I serve?
How shall I copy Him I love,
Nor from those blessed footsteps
swerve
Which lead me to His seat above?
2 Privations, sorrows, bitter scorn,
The life of toil, the mean abode,
The faithless kiss, the crown of
thorn,
Are these the consecrated road?
3 ‘Twas thus He suffered, though a Son,
Foreknowing, choosing, feeling all,
Until the perfect work was done,
And drunk the bitter cup of gall.
4 Lord, should my path through
suffering lie,
Forbid it I should e’er repine;
Still let me turn to Calvary,
Nor heed my griefs, rememb’ring
Thine.
5 Yes! I would count them all but loss,
That I may follow after Thee;
Flesh shrinks and trebles at the cross,
But Thou canst give the victory.
