Now the day is over
210  Eudoxia___C288, S296, F564 Lyndhurst___F564, C288

1 Now the day is over,
Night is drawing nigh,
Shadows of the evening,
Steal across the sky.
2 Now the darkness gathers,
Stars begin to peep,
Birds, and beasts, and flowers
Soon will be asleep!
3 Jesus, give the weary
Calm and sweet repose;
With Thy tender blessing
May mine eyelids close.
4 Grant to little children
Visions bright of Thee; Guard the sailors tossing On the deep blue sea.
5 Comfort every sufferer Watching late in pain; Those who plan some evil
From their sin restrain.
6 Through the long night-watches, May Thine angels spread
Their white wings above me, Watching round my bed.
7 When the morning wakens, Then may I arise
Pure, and fresh, and sinless In Thy holy eyes.
8 Glory to the Father, Glory to the Son,
And to Thee, blest Spirit, Whilst all ages run.
