According to Thy gracious word
229  Belmont___C309, S663, F540 Moravia___C306, F640

1 According to Thy gracious word,
In meek humility,
This will I do, my dying Lord,
I will remember Thee.
2 Thy body, broken for my sake,
My bread from heaven shall be;
Thy cup of blessing I will take,
And thus remember Thee.
3 Can I Gethsemane forget? Or there Thy conflict see,
Thine agony and bloody sweat, And not remember Thee?
4 When to the cross I turn mine eyes, And rest on Calvary,
O Lamb of God, my sacrifice, I must remember Thee.
5 And when these failing lips grow dumb,
And mind and mem’ry flee, When Thou shalt in Thy Kingdom,
come,
Jesus, remember me.
