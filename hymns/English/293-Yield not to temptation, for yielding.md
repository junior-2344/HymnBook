Yield not to temptation, for yielding
293     Fortitude___C704, S698

1 Yield not to temptation, for yielding
is sin;
Each victory will help you some
other to win;
Fight manfully onward, dark passions
subdue;
Look ever to Jesus, He will carry
you through.
Ask the Saviour to help you,
Comfort, strengthen, and keep you;
He is willing to aid you,
He sill carry you through.
2 Shun evil compassions, bad language
disdain;
God's Name hold in rev'rence, nor
take it in vain;
Be thoughtful and earnest,
kind-hearted and true;
Look ever to Jesus, He will carry
you through.
3 To him that o'ercometh God giveth
a crown;
Through faith we shall conquer,
though often cast down;
He, who is our Saviour, our strength
will renew;
Look ever to Jesus, He will carry
you through.
