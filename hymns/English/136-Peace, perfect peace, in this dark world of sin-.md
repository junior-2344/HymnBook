Peace, perfect peace, in this dark world of sin?

1 Peace, perfect peace, in this dark
world of sin?
The blood of Jesus whispers peace
within.
2 Peace, perfect peace, our future all
unknown?
Jesus we know, and He is on the
throne.
3 Peace, perfect peace, with loved
ones far away?
In Jesus' keeping we are safe, and they.
4 Peace, perfect peace, death shadow-
ing us and ours?
Jesus vanquished death and all its
powers.
5 It is enough; earth's struggles soon
shall cease,
And Jesus call us to heav'n's perfect
peace.
