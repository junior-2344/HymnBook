Immortal, invisible
361* St. Denio (Joanna)___F18, C12

1 Immortal, invisible,
God only wise,
In light inaccessible
Hid from our eyes,
Most blessed, most glorious,
The Ancient of Days,
Almighty, victorious,
Thy great Name we praise.
2 Unresting, unhasting,
And silent as light,
Nor wanting, nor wasting,
Thou rulest in might;
Thy justice like mountains
High soaring above
Thy clouds which are fountains
Of goodness and love.
3 To all, life Thou givest__ To both great and small;
In all life Thou livest, The true life of all;
We blossom and flourish As leaves on the tree, And witner and perish__
But nought changeth Thee.
4 Great Father of Glory, Pure Father of Light,
Thine angels adore Thee,
All veiling their sight;
All laud we would all render:
O help us to see
‘Tis only the splendor
Of light hideth Thee.
