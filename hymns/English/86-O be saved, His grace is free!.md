O be saved, His grace is free!
86         O be saved—S345

1 Sinner, how thy heart is troubled! God is coming very near;
Do not hide thy deep emotion, Do not check that falling tear.
Chorus
O be saved, His grace is free!
O be saved, He died for thee!
O be saved, he died for thee!
2 Jesus now is bending o'er thee Jesus lowly, meek, and mild; To the Friend who died to save thee, Wilt thou not be reconciled?
3 Art thou, waiting till the morrow? Thou may'st never see its light; Come at once! Accept His mercy; He is waiting – come to tonight!
4 Let the angels bear the tidings Upward to the courts of heaven! Let them sing, with holy rapture, O'er another soul forgiven!
