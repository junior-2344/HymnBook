Nothing either great or small

1 Nothing either great or small__
Nothing, sinner, no; 
Jesus did it, did it all, 
Long, long ago.
Chorus
“It is finished!” yes, indeed, 
Finished every jot:
Sinner, this is all you need__ 
Tell me, is it not?
2 When He, from His lofty throne, Stooped to do and die, Everything was fully done; Hearken to His cry.
3 Weary, working, burdened one, 
Wherefore toil you so?
Cease your doing; all was done Long, long ago.
