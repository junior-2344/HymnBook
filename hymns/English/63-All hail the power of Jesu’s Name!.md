All hail the power of Jesu’s Name!
63     Miles Lane—C139, F110

1 All hail the power of Jesu's Name! Let angels prostrate fall;
Bring forth the royal diadem, And crown Him, crown Him, crown Him!
Crown Him Lord of all.
2 Ye saints redeemed of Adam's race, Ye ransomed from the fall,
Hail Him who saves you by His grace, And crown Him, crown Him, crown Him!
Crown Him Lord of all.
3 Let every kindred, every tribe On this terrestrial ball,
To Him all majesty ascribe, And crown Him, crown Him, crown Him!
Crown Him Lord of all.
4 O that with yonder sacred throng We at His feet may fall,
Join in the everlasting song, And crown Him, crown Him, crown Him!
Crown Him Lord of all.
