There’s a Friend for the little
300      In memoriam___C593

1 There's a Friend for the little
children,
Above the bright blue sky,
A Friend that never changes,
Whose love will never die.
Unlike our friends by nature,
Who change with changing years,
This Friend is always worthy
The precious Name He bears.
2 There's a rest for little children
Above the bright blue sky,
Who love the blessed Saviour,
And the Father cry;
A rest from every turmoil
From sin and sorrow free,
Where e'ery little pilgrim
Shall rest eternally.
3 There's home for little children
Above the bright blue sky
Where Jesus reigns in glory,
A home of peace and joy.
No home on earth is like it,
Or can with it compare,
For every one is happy,
Nor could be happier, there.
4 There's a crown for little children
Above the bright blue sky,
And all who look to Jesus
Shall wear it by and by,
A crown of brightest glory,
Which He will then bestow
On all who love the Saviour,
And walk with Him below.
