There’s a Stranger at the door
369       Let Him in___S363

1 There’s a Stranger at the door:
Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
He has been there oft before:
Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
Let Him in ere He is gone;
Let Him in, the Holy One,
Jesus Christ, the Father’s Son:
Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
2 Open now to Him your heart:
Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
If you wait He will depart:
Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
Let Him in: He is you Friend;
He your soul will sure defend;
He will keep you to the end:
Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
3 Hear you now His loving voice?
Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
Now, O now make Him your choice:
Let Him In! (Let the Saviour in!)
He is standing at the door;
Joy to you he will restore, And His name you will adore:
Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
4 Now admit the heavenly Guest: Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!) He will make for you a feast: Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!) He will speak your sins forgiven;
And, when earthties all are riven, He will take you home to heaven: Let Him in! (Let the Saviour in!)
