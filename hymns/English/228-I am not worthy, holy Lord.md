I am not worthy, holy Lord
228     Hastings___S187, F431

1 I am not worthy, holy Lord,
That Thou shouldst some to me,
Speak but the word: one gracious
word
Can set the sinner free.
2 I am not worthy; cold and bare
The lodging of my soul;
How canst Thou deign to enter
there?
Lord, speak, and make me whole.
3 I am not worthy; yet, my God,
How can I say Thee nay,
Thee, who didst five Thy flesh and
blood
My ransom-price to pay?
4 O come, in this sweet hallowed hour,
Feed me with food divine;
And fill with all Thy love and power
This worthless heart of mine.
