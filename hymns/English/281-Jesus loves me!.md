Jesus loves me!
281      Jesus loves me___C660

1 Jesus loves me! This I know,
For the Bible tells me so;
Little ones to Him belong;
They are weak, but He is strong.
Yes! Jesus loves me!
Yes! Jesus loves me!
Yes! Jesus loves me!
The Bible tells me so.
2 Jesus loves me! He who died
Heaven’s gate to open wide;
He will wash away my sin,
Let His little child come in.
3 Jesus, take this heart of mine,
Make it pure and wholly Thine; Thou hast bled and died for me,
I will henceforth live for Thee.
4 Jesus loves me! He will stay Close beside me all the way; Then His little child will take
Up to heav’n, for His dear sake.
