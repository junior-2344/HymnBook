Come, Holy Spirit, come
68      Franconia—C190, F42

1 Come, Holy Spirit, come;
Let Thy bright beans arise;
Dispel all sorrow from our mind,
All darkness from our eyes.
2 Cheer our desponding hearts,
Thou heavenly Paraclete;
Give us to lie with humble hope
At our Redeemer’s feet.
3 Revive our drooping faith;
Our doubts and fears remove;
And kindle in our breasts the flame
Of never-dying love.
4 Convince us to our sin;
Then lead to Jesus’ blood,
And to our wond’ring view reveal
The secret love of God.
5 ‘TisThine to cleanse the heart,
To sanctify the soul;
To pour fresh life in every part,
And new-create the whole.
6 Dwell therefore in our hearts; 
Our minds from bondage free;
Then we shall know, and praise, and love
The Father, Son, and Thee.
