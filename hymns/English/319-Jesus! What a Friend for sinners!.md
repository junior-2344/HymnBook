Jesus! What a Friend for sinners!
319     Hyfrydol___C479, F113

1 Jesus! What a Friend for sinners!
Jesus! Lover of my soul;
Friend may fail me, foes assail me,
He, my Saviour, makes me whole.
Hallelujah! What a Saviour!
Hallelujah! What a Friend!
Saving, helping, keeping, loving,
He is with me to the end.
2 Jesus! What a Strength in weakness!
Let me hide myself in Him;
Tempted, tried, and sometimes failing,
He, my Strength, my vict’ry wins.
3 Jesus! What a Help in sorrow!
While the billows o’er me roll, Even when my heart is breaking, He, my comfort, helps my soul.
4 Jesus! What a Guide and Keeper! While the tempest still is high, Storms about me, night o’ertakes me,
He, my Pilot, hears my cry.
5 Jesus! I do now receive Him, More than all in Him I find,
He hath granted me forgiveness, I am His, and He is mine.
