There’ll be no parting
194      Heavenly Land___S991

1 I love to think of the heav’nly land,
Where white-robed angles are,
Where many a friend is gathered safe
From fear, and toil, and care.
There’ll be no parting,
There’ll be no parting, There’ll be no parting, There’ll be no parting there.
2 I love to think of the heav’nly land, Where my Redeemer reigns, Where rapturous songs of triumph
rise
In endless joyous strains.
3 I love to think of the heav’nly land, The saints’ eternal home,
Where palms, and robes and crowns
ne’er fade,
And all our joys are one.
4 I love to think of the heav’nly land, That promised land so fair;
O how my raptured spirit longs To be forever there!
