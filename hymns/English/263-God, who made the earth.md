God, who made the earth
263        Beechwood___C20

1 God, who made the earth,
The air, the sky, the sea,
Who gave the light its birth,
Careth for me.
2 God, who made the sun,
The moon, the stars, is He
Who, when life’s clouds come on,
Careth for me.
3 God, who made all things, On earth, in air, in sea,
Who changing seasons brings, Careth for me.
4 God, who sent His Son To die on Calvary,
He, if I lean on Him, Will care for me.
5 When in heav’n’s bright land I all His loved ones see,
I’ll sing with that blest band,
“God cared for me.”
