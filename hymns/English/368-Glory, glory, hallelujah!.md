1 Precious Saviour, Thou has saved me;
Thine, and only Thine, I am:
O the cleansing blood that reached
me!
Glory, glory to the Lamb!
Chorus
Glory, glory, hallelujah!
Glory, glory to the Lamb!
O the cleansing blood has reached me!
Glory, glory to the Lamb!
2 Long my yearning heart was striving To obtain this precious rest;
But, when all my struggles ended, Simply trusting, I was blest.
3 Consecrated to Thy service, I will live and die to Thee;
I will witness to Thy glory, Of salvation, full and free.
4 Glory to the Lord who bought me, Glory for His saving power;
Glory to the Lord who keeps me, Glory, glory evermore!
