Power in the blood
367   Power in the blood___S145

1 Would you be free from your burden
of sin?
There’s pow’r in the blood, pow’r in
the blood;
Would you o’er evil a victory win?
There’s wonderful pow’r in the blood.
There is power, power, wonder-work-
ing power,
In the blood of the Lamb;
There is power, power, wonder-work-
ing power,
In the precious blood of the Lamb.
2 Would you be free from your passion
and pride?
There’s pow’r in the blood, pow’r in
the blood;
Come for a cleansing to Calvary’s
tide.
There’s wonderful pow’r in the blood.
3 Would you be whiter, much whiter than snow?
There’s pow’r in the blood, pow’r in the blood;
Sin-stains are lost in its life giving flow,
There’s wonderful pow’r in the blood.
4 Would you do service for Jesus you King?
There’s pow’r in the blood, pow’r in the blood;
Would you live daily His praises to sing?
There’s wonderful pow’r in the blood.
