No room in the inn
91      Fortitude—S698, C704

1 No beautiful chamber,
No soft cradle bed,
No place but a manger,
Nowhere for His head;
No praises of gladness,
No thought for their sin,
No glory but sadness,
No room in the inn.
Chorus
In room, no room for Jesus!
O give Him welcome free,
Lest you should hear at heaven's
gate,
There is no room for thee!
2 No one to receive Him,
No welcome while hare,
No balm to relieve Him,
No staff but a spear;
No seeking His treasure,
No weeping for sin,
No doing His pleasure,
No room in the inn.
