“Abide in me!” Most loving counsel
152  Eventide___C286, S297, F560

1 “Abide in me!” Most loving counsel
this,
Nearest approach on earth to
heav’nly bliss;
With the command, O Saviour, give
me power
To live by faith on Thee, from hour
to hour.
2 “Abide in Me!” No ill can hurt
theethere;
In Me thou art safe even from the
tempter’s snare;
Before his fiery darts o’er thee
prevail
My life must end, My faithfulness
must fail.
3 “Abide in Me!” For I have strength
to give,
The grace to make thee henceforth
heav’nward live;
Eternal things My Spirit can reveal,
And thy heart’s earthly dark diseases
heal.
4 “Abide in Me!” If thou wouldst fruitful
be;
The branch bears not when sever’d from the tree;
Without My Spirit’s pow’r the sapless bough
No fruit can bear, for it can nothing do.
5 “Abide in Me!” All grace is Mine to give;
My voice the dead shall hear, and hearing live!
My Spirit can thy strongest sins subdue,
Soften thy heart, and all thy thoughts renew.
