Lord Jesus, I long to be perfectly
162    Whiter than Snow___S569

1 Lord Jesus, I long to be perfectly
whole,
I want Thee for ever to live in my
soul;
Break sown e’ery idol, cast out
e’ery foe.
Now wash me, I shall be whiter
than snow.
Whiter than snow, yes, whiter than
snow,
Now wash me, and I shall be whiter
than snow.
2 Lord Jesus, let me nothing unholy
remain,
Apply Thine own blood and extract
e’ery stain;
To get this blest cleansing I all things
forego
Now wash me, I shall be whiter
than snow.
3 Lord Jesus, look down from Thy
throne in the skies,
More hope in His Word: More tears for His sorrows, More pain at His grief, More meekness in trial, More praise for relief.
3 More victory give me,
More strength to o’ercome, More freedom from earth-stains, More fit for the kingdom,
More useful I’d be,
More blessed and holy.
More, Saviour, like Thee.
