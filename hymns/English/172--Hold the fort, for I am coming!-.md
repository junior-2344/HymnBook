172      Hold the Fort___S669

1 Ho, my comrades! See the signal
Waving in the sky!
Reinforcements now appearing,
Victory is nigh!
Chorus
"Hold the fort, for I am coming!"
Jesus signals still;
Wave the answer back to heaven,
"By Thy grace we will!"
2 See the might host advancing,
Satan leading on;
Mighty men around us falling,
Courage almost gone!
3 Fierce and long the battle rages,
But our help is near;
Onward comes our great Com-
mander;
Cheer, my comrades, cheer!
4 See the glorious banner waving!
Hear the trumpet blow!
In our Leader's name we triumph
Over every foe!
