More holiness give me
161 *       Longstaff___S608

1 More holiness give me,
More sweetness within,
More patience in suff’ring,
More sorrow for sin:
More faith in my Saviour,
More sense of His care,
More joy in His service,
More purpose in prayer.
2 More gratitude give me,
More trust in the Lord,
More zeal for His glory,
More hope in His Word: More tears for His sorrows, More pain at His grief, More meekness in trial, More praise for relief.
3 More victory give me,
More strength to o’ercome, More freedom from earth-stains, More fit for the kingdom,
More useful I’d be,
More blessed and holy.
More, Saviour, like Thee.
