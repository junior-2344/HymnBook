There is life for a look at the Crucified
81       Life for a look—S123

1 There is life for a look at the Cruci-fied One,
There is life at this moment for thee;
Then look, sinner, look unto Him
and be saved,
Unto Him who was nailed to the tree.
Look! Look! Look and live!
There is life for a look at the Cruci-
fied One;
There is life at this moment for thee.
2 O why was He there as the Bearer
of sin,
If on Jesus thy guilt was not laid?
O why from His sinde flowed the
sin-cleansing blood,
If His dying thy debt has not paid?
3 It is not thy tears of repentance or
prayers,
But the Blood, that atones for the
soul;
On Him, then, who shed it, thou
mayest at once
Thy weight of iniquities roll.
4 Then take with rejoicing from Jesus at once,
Take the life everlasting He gives; And know with assurance, thou
never canst die,
Since Jesus, thy Righteousness, lives.
