The morning bright
304          Sawley___F633

1 The morning bright,
With rosy light,
Has waked me up from sleep;
Father, I own,
Thy love alone
Thy little one doth keep.
2 All through the day,
I humbly pray,
Be Thou my Guard and Guide;
My sins forgive,
And let me live,
Blest Jesus, near Thy side.
3 O make Thy rest
Within my breast,
Great Spirit of all grace;
Make me like Thee,
Then shall I be
Prepared to see Thy face.
