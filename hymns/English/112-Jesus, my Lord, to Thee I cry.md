Jesus, my Lord, to Thee I cry
112     Take me as I am___S476

1 Jesus, my Lord, to Thee I cry; Unless Thou help me, I must die: O bring Thy free salvation nigh, And take me as I am.
Chorus
And take me as I am,
And take me as I am;
My only plea – Christ died for me! O take me as I am!
2 Helpless I am, and full of guilt; But yet for me Thy blood was spilt, And Thou canst make me what Thou wilt,
And take me as I am.
3 No preparation can I make, My best resolves I only break, Yet save me for Thine own Name's sake,
And take me as I am.
4 Behold me, Savior, at Thy feet, Deal with me as Thou seest meet; Thy work begin, Thy work complete, But take me as I am.
