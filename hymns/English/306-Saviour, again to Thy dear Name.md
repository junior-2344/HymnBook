Saviour, again to Thy dear Name
306    Ellers___C301, S291, F280

1 Saviour, again to Thy dear Name we
raise,
With one accord, our parting hymn
of praise;
We stand to bless Thee ere our
worship cease,
Then, lowly kneeling, wait Thy word
of peace.
2 Grant us Thy peace upon our
home-ward way;
With Thee began, with Thee shall
end the day;
Guard Thou the lips from sin, the
hearts from shame,
That in this house have called upon
Thy name.
3 Grant us Thy peace, Lord, through
the coming night,
Turn Thou for us its darkness into
light;
From harm and danger keep Thy
children free,
For dark and light are both to Thee.
4 Grant us Thy peace throughout our earthly life,
Our balm in sorrow, and our stay in strife;
Then, when Thy voice shall bid our conflict cease,
Call us, O Lord, to Thine eternal peace.
