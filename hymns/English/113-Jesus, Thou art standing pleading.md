Jesus, Thou art standing pleading
113 Stephanos___F279, C391, S401

1 Jesus, Thou art standing pleading,
Calling me to rest;
Shall I, yielding hearken to Thee,
And be blest?
2 Thou hast form’d me for Thy glory,
For Thine own delight;
Can I still withhold, Lord, from Thee,
This Thy right?
3 O this heart is weary, restless,
Bound by many a chain
Against which heart and will, though
stubborn,
Strive in vain.
4 Lord, I yield: no more withstanding
Thine all-loving will,
Take me, Master, break me, make me,
Cleanse and fill.
5 Take my will: ‘tis Thine henceforth, Lord,
Lead me by Thy way;
Let my words and actions please
Thee Day by day.
6 O the joy of full surrender, Keeping from Thee nought,
As I yield, my heart is finding Peace long sought.
